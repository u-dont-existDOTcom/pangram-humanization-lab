## Authorized editorial changes

### Card-game intimacy versus lived evidence

Location: `Starting on the right foot` -> `The window of clarity, and how it closes`.

Preserve the existing prepared-answer limitation and ordinary-time conclusion. Add the missing thought that a card game itself can generate a feeling of extraordinary closeness that proves the conversation worked, not that the relationship will work. Desired movement: **Talk -> observe in ordinary life -> revisit**. Avoid saying the same point twice.

### Crucible boundary for unilateral coercion

Location: near the opening of `Are you ready for the crucible?`, after the approach is introduced and before relational pressure is treated as growth opportunity.

Add a short warning that not every bad relationship is mutual triggering. If one person is terrorizing/controlling the other, or the other is afraid to say no/tell the truth/leave, do not turn it into a mutual communication exercise; involve other people and think about safety first. This bounds the crucible argument rather than replacing it.

### Labels without magical force

Location: situationship passage beginning `But here's the funny thing...`.

Revise only the opening thought so labels do not create commitment but can expose whether both people think they are in the same relationship. Preserve the downstream claim that true commitment grows out of relational depth and that responsibility can exist before or without a label.

### Early community seed

Location: end of `Things get tricky fast`.

Add a compact seed clarifying that community means people who actually know both partners over time, not separate friend camps hearing each side. Keep `Two Pillars Don't Hold The Roof Up` as the full explanatory home. Do not duplicate its mechanisms early.

### Readiness threshold in both senses of parenthood

Location: `Should you be in a relationship at all?`, immediately after the categorical `You're not ready.` statement.

Clarify that complete healing is not required; the present-day adult must be able to parent what intimacy wakes up internally and must be responsible enough to deal with an actual child if sex creates one. If a person cannot be a parent in either sense, they are not ready to date.

Audit the later `Nobody has to be completely healed` callback so it cannot be read as weakening the threshold.

### Bundling as imperfect historical example

Location: near the end of `How to find a partner`, before `Starting on the right foot`.

Use only if the history can be stated compactly and accurately. Bundling was not an Amish invention; related bed-courtship forms existed elsewhere. Historical Amish practice is real but was already described by a 1940s Amish study as a former practice rather than a present-day church custom. Broader eighteenth-century evidence describes courting couples sharing a bed, often clothed, under an expectation of no intercourse; high premarital-pregnancy prevalence in places where bundling was practiced shows the containment rule often failed, but does not establish bundling as the cause.

Conceptual job: illustrate the missing intermediate social form between interest and sexual/life entanglement. Omit rather than open a history tangent if the example cannot stay compact.

### Idealization -> ordinary dependability bridge

Location: `Beware idealization in either direction`, before the section turns toward slowing down/real-world observation.

Add a compact bridge from spiritual depth/beautiful answers/archetypes to ordinary adult dependability: boring work, illness, and promises. Leave the detailed dependability questions in `The conversation about flaws`.

### Bee door correction

Location: `If you're already in it`.

Control meaning:
- Bee did lie;
- `she lied` accurately names the momentary behavior;
- the label alone does not explain it;
- no strategy plus her usual honesty makes ordinary calculated dishonesty a poor complete explanation;
- possible mental illness might have led to more understanding without erasing the lie.

Never replace this with `I'm not sure lying is the right word.`

### Honesty setup only if audit demonstrates need

The article already contains privacy/timing/confidant/excess-honesty/radical-honesty distinctions. Add a short seed before the radical-honesty line only if continuous reading shows the governing distinction is unclear. Otherwise record `no change`.

### Vows heading and opening

Change heading from `Why marriage vows are dishonest` to `Which marriage vows are honest?`.

Opening may sharpen the existing movement: conventional vows are dishonest; future feeling cannot be promised; actions may be promised when the speaker believes they can deliver them. Preserve the existing scripture discussion, historical claim, attraction-switch experience, jealousy analysis, and monogamy/polyamory distinction. Do not rewrite the section as if feelings-versus-conduct had been absent.

### MDMA warning before attraction

Location: opening of `Psychedelics in relationship discernment`, before positive possibilities.

Put the warning before the attractive altered-state material: consent cannot be negotiated on MDMA because the drug is altering feelings of safety/love/openness/connection; there is no safe procedure for getting high and beginning a relationship; the intimacy can be real while saying little about sober ordinary-life functioning.

Preserve Bee/Key experiences, telepathic-feeling understanding, state-dependent learning, failure to integrate, sober non-combative communication as the practical test, Key/iboga, MDMA-party examples, ordinary-life evidence, and the `beyond all that` problem.
