Then there’s romantic love: I want you. I want your attention, your body, your specialness, your life intertwined with mine. There’s nothing fake or shameful about that. Romantic love is one of the most powerful things we get. But it is, by its nature, about what I want from one particular person.

A good relationship can hold both. Trouble starts when the romantic part is enormous and the selfless part is mostly decorative. Then I may adore how you make me feel, what you give me, even who I get to be beside you — while caring much less about what is actually best for you.
