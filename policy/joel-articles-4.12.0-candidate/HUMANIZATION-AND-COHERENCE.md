# Humanization and Coherence — Reconstruction Protocol

This file governs P2S, P3, P4, author-intent recovery, and detector repair for Joel's byline. Read it after `MASTER-INSTRUCTIONS.md` and `TASK-MODES.md`, and before drafting from interviews, notes, rough prose, or an AI-shaped source.

## The actual target

The target is not “text containing more of Joel's words.” It is **Joel's actual thought, organized into coherent finished prose that follows the route by which the thought makes sense**.

A passage can fail in either direction:

- **model normalization:** the propositions survive, but the model replaces discovery, contradiction, causality, evidence encounter, judgment, and uncertainty with balanced explanatory prose;
- **transcript dependence:** Joel's raw language survives, but the draft follows answer order, copies clauses, accumulates examples, and never becomes an article.

Both are fidelity failures. Humanization and composition are one task: recover the authorial reasoning, then write the best coherent version of it.

## 0A. Blocking execution order: sanity → curiosity → draft → cold audit

Do not start by rewriting merely because the local task says “humanize,” “fix,” or “redraft.” The following sequence is mandatory.

### Semantic sanity gate

Before prose, read the exact passage and its neighbors as an argument, not as text to improve. Ask:

- What exactly is being claimed or questioned?
- Does the thought itself make sense?
- Does a premise already answer the supposed question?
- Is a contradiction being manufactured by an unstated assumption?
- Is the prose assuming a separate actor, chooser, cause, motive, chronology, or ontology that was never established?
- Does the heading promise the question the passage is actually answering?
- Should this paragraph exist in anything like its inherited form?

If this gate fails, do not draft. Repair/recover the thought first. Ask Joel only for genuinely missing authorial information, not for information that is already available but poorly realized.

### Curious-reader chain

During reading and drafting, imagine that you personally care about the subject. After every sentence ask:

> **What do I actually want to know next?**

Let the next sentence answer, complicate, or redirect that live question. If the draft instead supplies the next slot in a pre-completed outline, the chain has failed. If the reader's curiosity has ended, that is often the stopping point. Do not manufacture a recap, moral, transferable principle, or transition merely because a section “needs an ending.” Do not turn this into fake suspense or a metronomic question-answer device.

This test also governs lists. A list is natural when the reader genuinely wants the next item or the list itself is the real object. Stop when the point is already understood; do not complete a taxonomy or questionnaire merely for coverage.

### Completed-output cold audit

Never assume generation-time compliance. After drafting, stop and inspect the literal completed passage as if somebody else wrote it. Run at least one full cold audit and normally two; run a third when the second still finds real defects. A pass may correctly make no changes. Stop when no legitimate defect remains.

Each pass checks:

1. semantic sanity again;
2. curious-reader continuity sentence by sentence;
3. unnecessary recap, moral, takeaway, or interpretive aftercare;
4. **functional redundancy**, even when two sentences use different words;
5. exhaustive checklists, question batteries, or complete conceptual classes;
6. predictable content-writer sequences such as `problem → explanation → example → caveat → synthesis` or `source A → role A; source B → role B; neat integration`;
7. generic bridges and meta-explanation that merely tell the reader why material matters;
8. natural, semantically faithful realization rather than neutral questionnaire language;
9. false symmetry and equal treatment not warranted by Joel's actual emphasis;
10. fidelity: claims, certainty, actor → action → object, chronology, causality, attribution, source roles, links, memories, and owner corrections;
11. the earliest honest stopping point.

Detector green is not a stop condition for this audit. Conversely, do not apply the audit as a ritual that forces changes to already-good prose.

### Reconstruct rather than polish when the architecture is the problem

If cold audits keep producing nicer versions of the same globally model-shaped argument, stop. Recover the richest available Joel/source pool and reconstruct from the real thought route. Do not substitute an AI-compressed semantic ledger for the actual memories, frustrations, source encounters, contradictions, examples, uncertainty, and unequal importance when those materials exist.

## 1. Separate archival fidelity from publication wording

Preserve Joel's raw interview answers, notes, corrections, exact memories, and rejected alternatives in the source ledger or carry-forward bank. That archival requirement does **not** make every raw sentence publication wording.

Exact wording belongs in the article when at least one of these is true:

1. Joel has made it owner-final or explicitly locked it;
2. it is an exact quotation, memory, formula, title, joke, coined term, or catchphrase whose wording is part of the evidence or identity;
3. it is distinctive author language that performs a necessary rhetorical job better than a fresh sentence would;
4. changing it would alter certainty, actor assignment, chronology, causality, emotional judgment, or the reader relationship.

Raw wording that is repetitive, answer-shaped, fragmentary, redundant, or merely traceable should be preserved in the record and synthesized in the prose. “Preserve exact raw wording” means **do not lose it**; it does not mean **copy it into the article**.

Owner-final prose is different. Do not rewrite an owner-final passage merely to demonstrate synthesis.

## 2. Build the coherence architecture before writing sentences

Before prose, create a private **coherence architecture card**. Use plain notes rather than polished sentences, and do not copy the source's sentence order.

Record:

- **Heading-function promise:** What exact question does the current heading promise to answer? Is the proposed passage answering that question or an adjacent one?
- **Real pressure or question:** What problem, contradiction, danger, desire, or uncertainty makes the section necessary?
- **Reader stake:** Why does this reader need it here, in this article?
- **Controlling claim:** What is Joel actually saying, at what certainty?
- **Motive or obligation, when relevant:** What reluctance, cost, conflict, duty, or practical pressure makes Joel write or publish this now? Why is he the person left to do it?
- **Intellectual or lived route:** What did he know first? What bothered him? What did he try, notice, remember, read, reject, or change his mind about? What did later research merely sharpen rather than originate? Where does he stop?
- **Actor → action → object:** Who does what to whom or what, and for what purpose?
- **Causal or chronological chain:** What has to precede what? Which relationship is causal, evidentiary, contrasting, or merely adjacent?
- **Source landscape and unequal roles:** What broad failure modes, traditions, or kinds of source define the field? Which source, person, example, memory, quotation, opponent, or experience actually changed Joel and deserves disproportionate space? What is only corroboration?
- **Mistaken deference or revised self-attribution:** Did Joel initially blame his own understanding, defer to another person's profundity, or mislocate confusion, authority, or responsibility? What changed?
- **Strongest complication:** What contradiction, mixed motive, unresolved alternative, or cost must remain active?
- **Governing movement:** Inquiry, lived sequence, causal pressure, contradiction, practical threshold, evidence encounter, comparison, instruction, or another movement native to the material.
- **Paragraph jobs:** What changes for the reader in each paragraph?
- **Stopping point:** Where has the thought arrived? What would become recap, moral, or explanatory aftercare if added?
- **Exact language to retain:** Which spans are exact, and why?

Do not let an inherited assistant heading, prior outline, question sequence, source order, or detector shading decide this architecture. Preserve them only when they remain correct after the semantic reset.

## 2A. Make the heading control the recovered question

Before author-intent questions, state the literal question the heading asks. A fluent passage can be about the right topic while performing the wrong section function.

A heading such as **“Why I’m publishing this”** or **“Why I’m writing this”** normally calls for motive, reluctance, cost, obligation, timing, and why Joel rather than someone else is left to speak. A novelty claim, literature survey, thesis preview, or orderly list of predecessors may belong inside that route, but it cannot replace it. Reject the draft when the heading promises motive and the passage answers only “What is new here?”

Do not assume the section needs a formative research encounter. Distinguish:

- what Joel already knew from years of experience or reading;
- what a later search confirmed;
- what it made more precise;
- what it revealed as closer than he remembered;
- and what remained far from the full synthesis.

Author-intent questions should recover this chronology without manufacturing an epiphany. Useful questions include: What made writing feel necessary despite reluctance? What did the broad classes of sources fail to provide? Who came closest and why did that person matter personally? Did Joel initially blame himself for not understanding? What later changed his judgment about the source, himself, or the distribution of confusion?

For source-rich sections, map the field before listing names. The real architecture may be monks constrained from reporting experience, lay teachers reporting experience without a matching textual account, and one personally consequential teacher who came closest but remained confused. That source world is not equivalent to five names receiving five equally polished contributions.

Humanization may therefore require substantial expansion. Restore the material that makes the conclusion necessary: reluctance, duration, failed source classes, dependence, admiration, self-doubt, changed attribution, and the strange position of being left with the synthesis. Do not simplify or delete an exact abstract ending merely because compressed assistant versions made abstraction look suspicious. An unusual conclusion can remain fully human-shaped when the route to it has been earned.

## 3. One governing movement can carry several functions

A section need not have only one function. It may simultaneously provide research transparency, component support, novelty evidence, personal provenance, humor, and practical consequence. The test is whether those functions participate in one intelligible movement.

Reject both extremes:

- **function flattening:** forcing a genuinely multi-function passage into one label;
- **model accumulation:** giving every source or answer its own paragraph and calling the resulting inventory “multi-function.”

The section should have one governing progression, not necessarily one job.

## 4. Draft from the architecture card, then return to the raw source

Use this sequence:

1. Complete the semantic reset and source-pool disposition.
2. Build the coherence architecture card without drafting prose.
3. Temporarily work from the card, claim map, and evidence-role map rather than line-by-line from the raw answers.
4. Draft the passage in fresh syntax.
5. Return to the raw source and restore exact lines whose wording is locked, evidentiary, identity-bearing, or functionally superior.
6. Run the omission, certainty, actor, chronology, link, and media audits.

This sequence prevents both generic paraphrase and clause-level collage. It is not a mandate to avoid Joel's language. It is a mandate to make the prose depend on his reasoning rather than on the physical order of his notes.

## 5. Run a verbatim-dependency audit

For every substantial span copied from an interview, brainstorm, rough note, or detector-repair answer, record a private reason:

- owner-final or explicitly locked;
- exact memory, quotation, formula, title, or source language;
- distinctive phrase whose wording performs necessary work;
- semantic precision that a rewrite would weaken;
- temporary source placeholder pending Joel's wording.

As a practical warning, any reused run of roughly twelve or more consecutive words from raw source material should receive a reason. This is an audit trigger, not a quota, plagiarism test, or prohibition. Short function words and inevitable formulations do not matter. Approved owner-final prose is exempt.

Fail the draft when it depends on copied clauses because the model could not decide how to organize the thought.

## 6. Test the paragraph chain

Each paragraph should have one intelligible main job, even when the section has several functions. For every paragraph, identify:

- what it inherits from the prior paragraph;
- whether the relation is cause, consequence, contrast, example, qualification, escalation, return, or decision;
- what pressure or question it creates for the next paragraph;
- what would be lost if it disappeared.

Warning signs:

- the only relation is “and Joel also said…”;
- paragraphs can be freely shuffled without changing the argument;
- each paragraph corresponds to one interview answer or one source;
- a paragraph exists only to announce, summarize, balance, defend, or preserve traceability;
- examples repeat the same moral instead of changing the case;
- the final paragraph merely restates the section.

A list or reference section may legitimately be modular. Judge modularity by genre and purpose, not by an automatic narrative preference.

## 7. Preserve the intellectual route

Proposition fidelity is not enough. Preserve how the thought was earned when that route supplies causality, authority, humility, or stakes:

- firsthand experience before later reading;
- frustration with existing explanations;
- a source that changed or failed to change Joel's view;
- a real opponent, tradition, person, or theory;
- a contradiction that remains unresolved;
- a search and the finding that would have stopped publication;
- exact uncertainty about what was not said, not found, or not established;
- self-implication alongside criticism.

Do not turn this into compulsory autobiography. Include only the route needed for the section's argument and reader relationship.

## 8. Let emphasis follow importance, not outline symmetry

Joel often gives more space to what changed his view, affected him, or must be seen directly. Do not equalize paragraph length, source treatment, objections, genders, traditions, or examples merely to make the section look complete.

Balanced symmetry is especially suspect when it:

- turns mixed motives into matched categories;
- creates reciprocal examples that did not matter to Joel;
- gives every alternative equal explanatory weight;
- delays the damaging extreme until after generic balance;
- manufactures one lesson per person or relationship;
- converts “it depends” into a complete decision tree.

Unevenness is not automatically human. It is correct when the evidence and authorial stakes are uneven.

## 9. Preserve contradiction, mixed judgment, and open causality

Do not solve tensions Joel means to retain. Examples from the comparison corpus include:

- sex can create and renew romance while sufficiently developed love may survive sexual loss;
- jealousy can reflect insecurity while the relationship may actually be insecure;
- a person can love someone and also want money, status, sex, children, or practical support;
- a relationship can nearly kill Joel without Bee having tried to kill him;
- a source can supply one useful idea while its wider worldview remains rejected;
- an unanswered question can show unresolved inquiry without supporting Joel's complete answer.

State the contradiction plainly when it is the architecture. Do not close it with a generic reconciliation.

## 10. Use concrete detail, humor, questions, and roughness only by function

Names, dialogue, jokes, slang, fragments, first person, rhetorical questions, odd analogies, and informal grammar are not humanization switches. They work when they carry evidence, diagnosis, causality, self-implication, pressure release, or reader contact.

Never import a successful device from another section. “Sweet treat or heroin,” the father/poster anecdote, fish in water, coloring books, turtles, Jenga, and lovey bunnies remain local unless the present thought independently calls for them.

Do not deliberately add errors or awkwardness. The goal is not lower polish; it is less model-determined thought.

## 11. Expansion and compression are both available

Expand when the assistant deleted the material that made the thought human and intelligible: causality, opponents, autobiography, exact dialogue, source history, evidence encounter, uncertainty, practical consequence, or the reason the section exists.

Compress when prose consists of scaffolding, symmetric examples, defensive hypotheticals, repeated explanation, branded framework recap, source-by-source inventory, or a conclusion after the conclusion.

Length is an outcome, not a humanization strategy.

## 12. Audit the stopping point independently of detectors

A good ending may be:

- a practical branch or action;
- an unresolved cost or contradiction;
- a direct addressee;
- a live wager, question, invitation, or limit;
- a concrete fact, joke, or preference that completes the movement;
- a deliberately plain sentence.

Do not add a summary, universal moral, balanced caveat, or inspirational close because the section “needs an ending.” Detector green does not prove the stopping point is good; detector orange does not authorize a worse ending.

## 13. Coherence gate before detector testing

Do not test a candidate until all are true:

- it works without consulting the interview or notes;
- it works with the actual preceding and following paragraphs;
- the claim, actor, cause, certainty, chronology, attribution, and source access are correct;
- the architecture is independent of question and source order;
- every paragraph changes the reader's understanding, pressure, evidence, or decision;
- exact raw wording is used only for a recorded reason;
- valuable unused material is dispositioned;
- the stopping point is deliberate;
- no model-written bridge, recap, metaphor, or moral is present merely to complete the form.

Detector work begins after this gate. It never substitutes for it.

## 13A. Triage local detector interference before replacing a coherent architecture

A coherent, faithful passage may still be classified differently after one contraction, caveat, phrase cluster, or list construction changes. Do not infer from one failed paraphrase that the paragraph's entire reasoning route is model-shaped.

After the coherence gate passes, preserve the strongest coherent baseline and test the smallest plausible local variables first:

1. natural contraction versus expanded negation;
2. idiomatic or nominal caveat versus a fully specified verbal disclaimer that manages every possible overreading;
3. flat coordinate syntax (`A, B, and C`) versus syntax that states the real relation among the identical items;
4. exact phrase order, sentence boundary, lead-in, and exit context.

For lists, separate cardinality from semantic topology. A three-item list is not inherently suspicious. The local risk shown in the comparison corpus is a flat canonical coordination whose items read as one polished conceptual class. The same scientific items may test differently when expressed as two related kinds of evidence rather than one tidy taxonomy; a three-item list with a genuine outlying item may also behave differently. Record the item set, apparent class, and syntax separately.

For caveats, separate contraction status from inferential completion. `They aren't proof of my experience` and `They don't prove what happened to me` are not the same editorial operation: the latter states the precise evidentiary inference more fully. Test both dimensions without assuming that contractions are human or that explicitness is AI.

Never add an irrelevant list item, delete true evidence, weaken a necessary distinction, or accept worse prose merely to disrupt a detector pattern. A local detector win counts only when fidelity, coherence, article function, and Joel's preference do not regress. If bounded tests fail and the architecture itself remains portable, symmetrical, or over-complete, then consider global reconstruction.

## 14. Detector and P1 rules

Track authorship, owner acceptance, coherence/fidelity, detector status, and publication authority separately.

Pure P1 corrections—spelling, punctuation, capitalization, spacing, literal agreement, and broken-link repairs that do not change visible semantic wording, sentence structure, paragraphing, order, emoji, link-anchor text, or tested boundaries—retain the prior detector status. Record the correction. Retest only after substantive visible or boundary changes when detector locking matters.

## 15. Required pass report

For a substantive humanization or reconstruction pass, report:

- **Heading-function fit:** literal promise answered / adjacent-question substitution;
- **Architecture card:** complete / incomplete;
- **Motive/obligation route:** recovered / not relevant / replaced by novelty summary;
- **Source landscape and weighting:** real failure modes + unequal roles / one-contribution-per-name inventory;
- **Governing movement:** named;
- **Intellectual route:** preserved / not relevant / replaced incorrectly;
- **Raw-language handling:** fresh synthesis / justified exact reuse / transcript dependence;
- **Verbatim dependencies:** zero / fully justified / unresolved;
- **Paragraph chain:** pass / fail;
- **Coherence in context:** pass / fail;
- **Fidelity:** pass / fail;
- **Contradictions and mixed judgments:** preserved / resolved with authority / flattened;
- **Stopping point:** deliberate / recap / unresolved;
- **Source-pool disposition:** complete / unresolved;
- **P1 status:** none / recorded without retest / substantive retest required;
- **Local detector-interference triage:** not needed / contraction-negation / caveat realization / list topology / phrase-order-boundary tested;
- **Detector evidence:** document / sentence / boundary, kept separate;
- **Largest remaining weakness:** exact location and reason.

## 16. Hard failure conditions

Reject the passage before delivery when any of these is true:

- it is a lightly connected collage of Joel's answers;
- the outline can be reconstructed from the question order;
- it preserves propositions while replacing Joel's intellectual route;
- the heading asks why Joel is writing or publishing, but the passage answers only what is novel;
- prior knowledge is rewritten as a source-triggered research epiphany;
- broad source failure modes, personal dependence, or mistaken deference are flattened into one contribution per name;
- each source receives a symmetrical paragraph regardless of importance;
- exact phrases are copied without a reason because the model did not compose;
- a real contradiction becomes a taxonomy or compromise;
- actor assignment or motive certainty changes;
- paragraphs are exchangeable modules when the section requires progression;
- the ending explains what the reader learned;
- the draft blames or simplifies an exact abstract conclusion when the missing defect is the unearned route leading to it;
- a coherent local passage is globally rebuilt after one detector failure without testing plausible contraction, caveat, list-topology, or boundary interference;
- content is added, removed, weakened, or made awkward solely to disrupt a detector pattern;
- detector improvement required worse writing, weaker fidelity, or random “human” quirks.

The standard is coherent, publication-quality prose that Joel recognizes as his thought—not a transcript with polish and not a model essay wearing his vocabulary.

## 17. Developmental diagnosis is separate from voice reconstruction

Whole-article diagnosis and prose reconstruction are distinct capabilities. The developmental pass identifies the article's promise, destination, pacing, hierarchy, credibility timing, skimmer path, recurrence, appendix division, and ending function without rewriting. The implementation pass performs only accepted operations. A good outside editor remains evidence rather than authority: record the recommendation, the concern beneath it, Joel's acceptance/rejection/qualification, the corrected controlling purpose, and analogous recommendations affected by that correction.

## 18. Record purpose, promise, distinctiveness, and payoff

Before global structural advice, state:

- the mystery, contradiction, or problem being solved;
- the article's destination;
- why the reader must pass through the earlier route;
- what counts as payoff;
- what the opening must promise without revealing prematurely.

A thriller-shaped middle may delay the answer. It may not delay the reason to care. Distinctive lived material should generally arrive before generic metaphysics, theory, or claims that demand a large expenditure of reader trust. This is sequencing, not automatic softening.

## 19. Treat hierarchy, recurrence, autonomy, and endings as reader architecture

For skimmers, the heading tree is the argument. Verify that heading levels and the table of contents display real importance and relationships. Each major section should remain intelligible to a reader arriving at its heading; preserve a concise repeated premise when it supplies necessary local orientation, and use a pointer when the full proof already exists elsewhere.

Judge repetition by function and reader state. Track the role of each appearance and what a skimmer would lose there. Prefer movement or pointing before deletion. Audit distinctive repeated terms for semantic valence: the same word may name a rejected claim in one place and an endorsed claim in another.

Audit endings with an ending-function card: intended reader action or intellectual state; the passage that actually performs it; whether that passage is visible to ordinary readers; and whether the current last passage is carrying a job it cannot do. The main article carries the central route, promise, payoff, and invitation or decision; a technical appendix carries the extended defense, source trail, philology, mechanisms, and receipts when those are its real audience and function.

## 20. Track four epistemic planes

Every substantial changed passage must distinguish:

1. **World claim:** what is claimed to be true.
2. **Author commitment:** what Joel believes and at what certainty.
3. **Inferential status:** observation, memory, quotation, established theory, interpretation, synthesis, mechanism, analogy, speculation, or invitation.
4. **Detector evidence:** classifier result for the exact boundary, model/version/date, and nothing more.

A detector result cannot change the first three. A citation cannot silently make an adjacent author inference look source-established. Mark the boundary in ordinary prose without turning the article into a legal memo.

## 21. Test falsifiability, abduction, and credibility timing

Originality, lost-key, and unfamiliarity claims require an anti-self-sealing check. Record what prior synthesis would have stopped publication, what evidence would refute the claim, what was searched, what remains unavailable, and whether absence is evidence, context, or neither.

Treat difficulty imagining a model as abductive support only when the model independently predicts that difficulty, the prediction was not invented after the observation, competing explanations were tested, and the wording says support or likelihood rather than proof.

Use a credibility-budget audit for high-cost commitments such as rebirth, contested textual readings, rare phenomenology, lost-tradition claims, and scientific extrapolation. Preserve Joel's conviction; sequence the claim and its evidence where a skeptical reader can evaluate them.

## 22. Preserve experience, mechanism, ontology, universality, and reader prediction separately

A first-person report may be categorical about what Joel experienced while remaining provisional about cause, mechanism, ontology, generalization, or what another person will experience. Audit sentence movement among:

- this happened to me;
- this caused it;
- this is what the phenomenon is;
- this occurs generally;
- this will happen to the reader.

Do not let one layer validate another by proximity.

## 23. Quality gates control detector work

A candidate is acceptable only when fidelity passes; coherence is at least as strong as the controlling baseline; editorial quality and article function do not regress; and Joel's preference does not regress when available. Only then may detector improvement count positively. Store human false positives, AI false negatives, and detector-green bad prose as controls rather than exceptions to erase.

For evaluation, preserve exact source → assistant → owner triples where available, full neighboring context, detector boundaries, conceptual corrections, rejected candidates, and Joel's quality judgment. Use a development set and frozen holdout. Blind fidelity/coherence/editorial ranking precedes detectors. Promote a rule only when it recurs across materially different cases and survives the holdout.

## 24. Propagate accepted corrections into one current master

Ledgers do not substitute for installation. After every accepted section change, update the current master or mark it `pending reconstruction`. Use this closure path:

**captured → classified → applied to master → verified in master → dependency audit complete → detector status determined → closed**

Distinguish `reference known`, `body partially retrieved`, `body fully retrieved`, `body installed`, and `body verified`. Do not call the master complete, current, or authoritative while it contains assembly-gap markers, missing authoritative section bodies, resurrected owner deletions, failed correction checks, unrecorded provenance/detector states, or unnamed source/link gaps. Deterministic validators may block completion for recurrent high-risk errors; they may not rewrite prose.


## 25. Preserve conversationally sufficient modifiers

Model polishing often replaces an ordinary but sufficient word with one that specifies the relationship more explicitly than the thought requires. Preserve `just`, `really`, `quite`, `somehow`, `even`, `still`, `probably`, and similar owner wording unless a substantive distinction requires a change.

Before substituting `exactly`, `precisely`, `specifically`, `particularly`, or another analytical synonym, check whether it:

- strengthens certainty or deliberateness;
- closes natural and accurate ambiguity;
- turns a remembered reaction into a completed analytical classification;
- makes the rhetorical relationship more explicit than Joel made it;
- replaces reported intellectual experience with retrospective explanatory architecture;
- formalizes or regularizes the surrounding rhythm.

This is not a vocabulary blacklist. The problem is unnecessary precision upgrade or analytical synonym over-explication. Prefer the owner's simpler wording when it already carries the intended thought.

## 26. Separate cold prose shape from provenance and quality

A short excerpt can express Joel's exact position faithfully and still be fully AI-shaped. When the task is classification rather than repair, evaluate prose shape before topic, accuracy, provenance, detector results, agreement, or epistemic quality.

Report independently:

1. prose shape;
2. thought provenance;
3. fidelity and editorial quality.

Use content-neutralization to remove subject-specific nouns and test the remaining architecture for portability. Do not count caution, accuracy, doctrinal specificity, calibrated uncertainty, fairness, or first-person grammar as human-shape evidence. In short analytical prose, inspect clusters of completed inference, nested hedge, immediate caveat, closure of overreadings, first-person wrapper without visible reasoning, and symmetrical claim/limitation. No single device decides the verdict.
