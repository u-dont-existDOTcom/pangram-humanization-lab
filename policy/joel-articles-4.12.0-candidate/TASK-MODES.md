# Task Modes and Preservation Contracts

Choose one primary mode and the least invasive level that satisfies Joel's request. The existing draft is evidence, not disposable raw material.

## Preservation levels

- **P0 — report only:** critique, audit, claim ledger, image plan, or reviewer analysis. Do not rewrite.
- **P1 — proof/copy edit:** spelling, grammar, punctuation errors, broken links, and obvious ambiguity. Preserve everything else.
- **P2 — line edit:** improve clarity, rhythm, repetition, and local voice fit with the smallest effective changes. Preserve argument, examples, order, emotional temperature, and memorable lines.
- **P2S — style-only reconstruction:** sentence architecture, paragraphing, transitions, humor, and section order may change; propositions, factual assignments, allegations, certainty markers, recommendations, links, media, coined terms, catchphrases, and personal history are locked.
- **P3 — developmental edit:** reorganize, combine, cut, or expand where authorized while preserving Joel's position, supplied personal material, best lines, controversial purpose, and evidentiary distinctions.
- **P4 — new draft/major rewrite:** construct new prose from the full supplied material. No invented persona.

Before P2S/P3/P4 on an existing article, read `EDIT-CONTRACT-AND-LEDGERS.md` and `HUMANIZATION-AND-COHERENCE.md`, establish substantive-change permission, and complete the coherence architecture card before drafting prose.

## Mandatory execution gate for P2S/P3/P4 and detector repair

Before any prose generation in these modes:

1. run the semantic sanity gate from `HUMANIZATION-AND-COHERENCE.md`;
2. decide whether the inherited architecture survives or must be rejected;
3. draft using the curious-reader chain (`What do I actually want to know next?` after each sentence);
4. run at least one completed-output cold audit and normally two before showing Joel or testing a detector;
5. stop rewriting when the audit finds no legitimate defect.

If the source thought itself is incoherent or underdetermined, do not “humanize” it. Recover the thought or ask the narrow irreducible authorial question first. If the architecture is globally model-shaped, reconstruct from rich author/source material rather than line-editing an AI outline. Detector green never waives fidelity/editorial review and detector red never authorizes worse prose.

## Audience contract — all modes

Before substantial drafting or editing, record the primary reader's role, expected knowledge, relationship to the writer/subject, intended action or decision, stakes, register, vocabulary allowance, and publication venue. Record deliberate section-level shifts. Preserve this contract unless Joel changes it. Audit role, expertise, goal, relationship, scope, action, register, assumed-knowledge, stakes, and venue drift; audience drift is not limited to therapy or health articles.

## New draft or section — P4

- Use the fullest notes, links, quotations, memories, contradictions, failed attempts, jokes, images, and prior prose.
- State the audience contract before the representative sample; ask only when two materially different audiences remain plausible.
- Infer informality, lightheartedness, humor, and sober zones. Ask only if the choice materially changes the article.
- For long or uncertain work, show one representative section first unless Joel asks for the complete draft now.
- Give a brief quality forecast and name the highest-leverage missing input.
- Do not manufacture a personal opening because another sample contains one.

## Style-only rewrite — P2S

- Build the fact-and-meaning ledger before rewriting.
- Preserve every substantive proposition and its certainty level.
- Do not browse, fact-check, add caveats, remove claims, strengthen evidence language, or change the author's position unless separately requested.
- Preserve exact memories, quotations, lyrics, jokes, catchphrases, coined terms, links, media, and entity assignments.
- Reordering requires a referent, chronology, and dependency audit.
- If a genuine ambiguity blocks the rewrite, ask about that passage rather than deciding what Joel meant. Draft from the architecture card in fresh syntax; preserve raw answers in the ledger and reuse substantial exact spans only for a recorded reason.

## Outline or developmental plan — P0/P3

- Offer the strongest architecture supported by the material.
- Give each section one governing movement. Several legitimate functions may coexist when they participate in that movement; do not force one narrow label or create a source-by-source inventory.
- Identify the strongest concrete material, serious objection, and honest ending move.
- Include a preliminary image opportunity map when visuals could carry evidence or pacing.

## Developmental edit or major rewrite — P3/P4

- Request current raw editor HTML when links, media, captions, native objects, paywalls, heading hierarchy, and formatting must survive. Extract the editor body only; exclude publication chrome.
- Track source wording/object, meaning and rhetorical function, required context, attribution, and destination separately.
- Diagnose selection, order, causality, evidence, framing, pacing, and ending before sentences.
- Before cutting or moving anything, record what job it performs. After every cut, insertion, or reorder, run the orphan audit in `EDIT-CONTRACT-AND-LEDGERS.md`.
- Reconstruct an AI-shaped draft from the ledger and source material; do not retain its rhetorical skeleton by inertia.
- Preserve central controversial values. When they determine compatibility, child-rearing, medicine, sexuality, religion, relationships, or daily trust, treat them as membership architecture rather than removable side debates.
- Preserve genuine praise while adding complications precisely; do not create nuance by reversing the praise.
- Keep geographic claims at the narrowest supported level.
- Allow an important issue to recur only when each appearance performs a different function; record those functions.
- Recheck `otherwise`, `also`, `still`, `other`, `same`, `too`, `yet`, and `but` after inserting or moving material.
- Ask for permission before adding, deleting, or materially changing claims.
- Whenever a section is consolidated, tell Joel what was combined, where each unique function went, and whether removed text was moved, duplicated, or superfluous.
- Calibrate one representative section before rebuilding a long article when practical.

## Line or copy edit — P1/P2

- Make the smallest effective changes.
- Preserve architecture, meaning, links, headings, jokes, punctuation habits, em-dashes, emojis, exact phrases, and emotional temperature. Pure P1 spelling, punctuation, capitalization, spacing, literal-agreement, and broken-link corrections retain prior detector status when visible semantic wording and tested boundaries do not change.
- Apply the exact redundancy rule and flag anything still academic, generic, or off-voice.
- A clean reconstruction is not a line edit.

## Critique or reviewer-feedback pass — P0

- Read `REVIEW-WORKFLOW-RULES.md`; report rather than rewrite unless repair is explicitly requested.
- Reconcile the complete comment history chronologically. Later corrections and retractions control.
- Treat each comment as a judgment signal; identify proposition, certainty, function, the full audience contract, required context, and dependencies.
- State each underlying concern once and list all recurrences.
- Separate must-fix issues, optional improvements, and reviewer taste.
- Identify what must survive repair and which completed audits need not be repeated.
- Maintain original-vs-current and, after repairs, previous-delivery-vs-corrected baselines.
- Use atomic semantic blocks and typed native-object placeholders under `REVIEW-INTERFACE-SPEC.md`.

## Fact-check/source pass — P0/P2

- Use a claim ledger and separate fact, mechanism, association, anecdote, speculation, and opinion.
- Follow the saved permission matrix before changing article text.
- List every authorized substantive change.

## Spoken transcript adaptation — P2/P3

- Preserve emotional stance, direct contact, purposeful repetition, humor, and reasoning style.
- Remove filler, false starts, loops, and speech-only tangents.
- Catchphrases that depend on intonation should be used only where the written context carries them.

## Fingerprint/AI-shape audit — P0

- Follow `FINGERPRINT-PASS.md`. For a short human/AI/mixed question, also use the short-excerpt anti-halo section in `FINGERPRINT-PASS.md` and the controlled minimal-pair ledger in `EDIT-CONTRACT-AND-LEDGERS.md`.
- Judge prose shape cold before topic, truth, provenance, detector results, agreement with Joel, or epistemic quality.
- Report prose shape, thought provenance, and fidelity/editorial quality separately; only prose shape controls the human/AI/mixed verdict.
- Apply content-neutralization and inspect portable claim–qualification architecture. Do not use percentages for very short excerpts unless requested.
- Report; do not rewrite or claim authorship proof.

## Visual pass — P0/P2

- Follow `VISUAL-EDITORIAL-PROTOCOL.md`.
- Identify visual opportunities, placement, type, purpose, source/generation path, caption, alt text, and evidence status.
- Do not move or replace existing media without authorization.

## Post-draft pass — required after P3/P4

- Run the voice, provenance, entity, coined-term, referent, rhetorical-function, required-context, orphan, scope, purposeful-recurrence, transition-word, audience-contract, jargon/plain-language, evidence, motif, review-comment, visual, heading-hierarchy, and table-of-contents checks. Include linked-reference necessity, new-explanation provenance, source-specific attribution, terminology bridges, independent redundancy after owner comments, semantic native-object placement, transfer conversion, and derivative compatibility regression when applicable.
- Surface only exact voice uncertainties.
- Provide the concise pass assessment in `QUALITY-FORECAST-AND-PASS-REVIEW.md` unless Joel requested final article only.

## Finalization — P2

- Preserve verified links, emphasis, headings, blockquotes, lists, captions, media islands, punctuation, and intentional emojis.
- Use `scripts/html_islands.py` for fragile Substack HTML.
- Return the complete semantic HTML as a downloadable file. Keep claim-change, uncertainty, image, and pass notes outside the file unless they belong in the publication itself.

## Surgical mode after approval

Once the article is strong, do not perform another broad rewrite by default. Change only the named headings, paragraph, joke, value statement, appendix label, link, or HTML defect. A source-article correction may require rebuilding derivative artifacts, but the derivative rebuild remains surgical: preserve every confirmed compatibility invariant and change only what the source correction invalidated. Provide a concise changed-passages list plus the complete updated article when Joel requests the final version.

## Substantial HTML revision delivery

For a substantial revision of an existing HTML article:

- read `PROJECT_STATE.md`, the last approved article, and the newest raw comments file before older branches; use the last approved article as the review baseline unless Joel explicitly chooses another;
- preserve intentional links, repeated links, media, captions, emphasis, lists, and section order unless the edit contract authorizes change;
- assign a monotonic `rNN-candidate` revision ID; do not label delivery as final or approved;
- return the complete authoritative candidate HTML file;
- when useful, also return clean article-only HTML without publication chrome;
- generate an interactive side-by-side review with `interactive_review.py`: one semantic block per cell; selected-text and whole-cell comments; Keep, Remove, Brainstorm, and reasoning; Humor, Technical detail, Length, and Bluntness controls; Copy JSON, Copy Markdown, JSON/Markdown downloads; local persistence; search; changed-only filtering; and the `joel-commentable-diff-review-v4`-compatible schema;
- for large articles, use `--changed-passages-only` after full semantic alignment so performance improves without dropping any review function;
- after a correction, generate the previous-delivery-vs-corrected baseline in addition to the original-vs-current baseline;
- use `html_diff.py` only for an optional quick static changed-passages view when Joel explicitly requests it; never use it as a substitute for the interactive review artifact;
- never render, open, embed, generate, or offer previews; provide files/download links only.
- package substantial review work in one authoritative ZIP containing the article, review file(s), immutable `review/source-comments.json`, `project/PROJECT_STATE.md`, changelog, README, manifest, checksums, and applicable comment/artifact/test ledgers; include a continuation handover only when an actual transfer is requested or planned; every full handoff includes section provenance, the active omission audit, and the assistant-produced recheck queue; record unavailable artifacts rather than reconstructing them.

## Review-artifact regression

The interactive review interface is a locked derivative artifact. Rebuilding the article or review must preserve its control set and order, side-by-side/stacked layout, one-block-per-cell semantics, Copy JSON, Copy Markdown, file exports, local persistence, `joel-commentable-diff-review-v4` compatibility, reasoning, search, filtering, and rhetoric sliders. Run `interactive_review.py --selftest`, `review_interface_browser_test.py` on the exact local file, and `review_package.py verify` on the exact ZIP. Follow `REVIEW-PACKAGE-REGRESSION.md`; a static yellow/collapsed diff or an earlier working copy is not an acceptable substitute.

## Artifact-family and HTML-output routing

- When a source passage changes, consult the artifact-family dependency ledger before declaring the pass complete. Update, defer, or intentionally omit every affected manual, app, diff, helper, self-hosted page, Ghost fragment, or sibling guide.
- A final Substack artifact with native objects includes a newly rebuilt minimal transfer helper and transfer report unless Joel declines them. Build a separate transfer-ready payload from the exact final archival editor body. For the confirmed Opera local-file path, prefer one `Copy Article` payload wrapped in `div[dir=auto].body.markup`; remove only known editor locks; keep images, previews, video, Share, and Subscribe in rich HTML; replace comments with canonical URLs at the same source positions; and show a paywall step only when a genuine marker exists. Compare with the last confirmed derivative; a matching source hash alone is insufficient.
- Choose the output mode explicitly: archival Substack HTML, transfer-ready payload/helper, self-hosted standalone HTML, or Ghost HTML-card fragment. Do not mix their constraints.
- For self-hosted mode, remove former-host dependencies and validate metadata, unique IDs, anchors, mobile, print, and interface-chrome removal.

## Research-and-argument mode — P0/P3/P4

Use for fact-checks, research-heavy essays, polemics, responses to videos/articles, contested biographies, institutional criticism, historical arguments, and evidence-based cultural commentary.

For substantial tasks:

1. extract the exact central claim and narrowest worthwhile conclusion;
2. identify load-bearing premises;
3. establish chronology;
4. retrieve the strongest sources;
5. test adverse, benign, and mixed interpretations symmetrically;
6. rank evidence by probative value;
7. assess each item alone and cumulatively;
8. derive the strongest fair conclusion and conclusion ladder;
9. document research/source-access limits;
10. only then derive the minimum decisive case and short forms.

Do not require a giant matrix for a simple factual claim. Use `ARGUMENT-AND-EVIDENCE-ARCHITECTURE.md`; add `CONTROVERSIAL-TOPIC-EVIDENCE-AUDIT.md` for documentary allegations and contested public figures.

## Deterministic ledger threshold and scope

Before running `argument_ledger.py`, classify task type and premise risk. State the proposed scope—none, minimal, standard, or full—and the reason. Ask Joel to choose only when two scopes are genuinely reasonable and the extra bookkeeping would materially affect time, complexity, or output; otherwise proceed with the least burdensome justified scope.

- **No full ledger by default:** ordinary essay, personal essay, simple explainer, P1/P2 copy/line edit, or P2S style-only reconstruction without a disputed premise.
- **Minimal:** one to roughly twelve material claims, or a small cluster where a correction could affect a few sections.
- **Standard:** research-heavy article or polemic with medium/high premise risk and several explicit dependencies.
- **Full:** investigation, contested documentary record, high-stakes allegation, or article family where premise failure could affect title, opening, conclusion, captions, short forms, apps, or sibling artifacts.

Use `python argument_ledger.py assess ...` when uncertain. Map only load-bearing premises and direct dependents. A mode cap is a warning to shrink scope, not a target. If the ledger begins mapping ordinary prose, stop and return to article-level judgment.

The script may index sources, add IDs, validate references, render the ledger, report explicit premise-failure impact, and render an explicitly selected minimum decisive case. It must not rewrite the article. Repair follows the task mode and permission matrix.


## Section-by-section continuation discipline

- Verify the complete source span and neighboring headings before declaring a section preserved, omitted, consolidated, or complete. Treat handoff excerpts as bounded targets.
- Locate the next section independently from the active article, Project Sources, File Library, or handoff packet before asking Joel to provide it.
- When the source already states the idea, summarize the existing claim and ask Joel to confirm, reject, or correct it. Do not make him repeat available material.
- When placement remains open, recommend the strongest destination and explain the tradeoff.
- Update section provenance, the cumulative omission audit, carry-forward destinations, and the assistant-produced recheck queue after every pass.
- End each section pass with the next source analysis or the exact next author-intent questions, not a generic request for more text.

## Worker-chat continuity boundary

A worker chat may handle successive closely related passes while the baseline and current branch remain clear. Do not create a ZIP or start a fresh chat merely because a pass changed authoritative state. Continue in the same worker by default; use the least burdensome handoff only when Joel requests a transfer, a materially separate outcome should move elsewhere, or context/branch ambiguity creates a real accuracy risk. Substantial review-family ZIP requirements remain delivery-specific and do not automatically trigger a handoff. Keep immediate repair in the current worker. Follow `WORKER-CHAT-HANDOFF-RULES.md`.

## AI-shape repair and author-intent recovery — P0/P2S/P3

Use `HUMANIZATION-AND-COHERENCE.md` and `TRANSFORMATION-CASE-STUDY.md`.

1. Classify the source: Joel-shaped with local model polish / mixed throughout / generated from the premise upward.
2. Separate mechanical defects—duplicate text, OCR/PDF corruption, lost punctuation or links, editor overlays, scan artifacts—from voice findings.
3. Run the semantic reset: claim, actor → action → object, cause, chronology, heading, attribution, link referent, source access, and whether the passage should survive.
4. Run the heading-function gate. State the literal question promised by the heading and reject an outline that answers a nearby but different question. For “Why I’m publishing/writing this,” recover reluctance, cost, obligation, why now, and why Joel; do not substitute a novelty summary or literature survey.
5. Distinguish prior knowledge from what a later search sharpened, verified, or made more precise. Do not invent a source-triggered epiphany.
6. Treat interviews and notes as source pools. Disposition every substantive item; archive exact raw wording without assuming it belongs verbatim in publication.
7. Build the coherence architecture card before sentences: heading promise, pressure, reader stake, claim, motive/obligation, intellectual/lived route, causal chain, source landscape and unequal evidence roles, complication, governing movement, paragraph jobs, stopping point, and exact-language reasons.
8. Build the outline independently of question order, source order, and inherited assistant architecture. One governing movement may carry several functions. In source-rich sections, organize the field by real failure modes, chronology, personal dependence, mistaken deference, and changed judgment—not one sealed contribution per name.
9. Draft in fresh syntax from the architecture card, then restore only exact language that is locked, evidentiary, identity-bearing, semantically precise, or functionally superior.
10. Run the verbatim-dependency audit. Reject clause mosaics, answer-order paragraphs, and substantial copied spans without a reason.
11. Run the paragraph-chain test and read the candidate without the source pool, then with the actual preceding and following paragraphs. Confirm deliberate stopping point.
12. Preserve contradiction, mixed motive, unequal emphasis, exact uncertainty, real opponent/source world, evidence encounter, self-implication, and open causality when they are the thought.
13. Use expansion to restore omitted causality, autobiography, publication motive, source history, dependence, mistaken deference, and stakes; use compression to remove scaffolding, symmetry, defense, and recap. Do neither by detector superstition. An earned abstract conclusion may remain intact.
14. Treat names, first person, jokes, questions, fragments, slang, metaphors, and paragraph length as local functional choices—not humanization switches.
15. Test detectors only after coherence and fidelity pass. Keep document, sentence, and boundary signals separate.
16. If a coherent local passage fails, preserve the strongest baseline and run controlled local-interference tests before replacing the architecture: contraction versus expanded negation; nominal or idiomatic caveat versus fully specified verbal disclaimer; flat `A, B, and C` coordination versus relation-aware syntax with the same items; exact phrase order and boundary. Change one visible variable at a time. Three-item lists, contractions, and ordinary phrases are not universal signals. Never add, delete, or distort content solely to obtain a detector score.
17. Lock only exact coherent owner-approved units and relevant boundaries. Pure P1 corrections retain prior detector status; substantive visible or boundary changes require a new result when locking matters.
18. Preserve every candidate and revert on regression. Permit one coherent post-interview architecture and one genuinely different architecture test only after local interference has been ruled out where applicable; after two genuine global failures, request Joel's rewrite.
19. Return complete paste-ready prose and report heading-function fit, architecture, local-interference triage, raw-language handling, verbatim dependencies, paragraph chain, source-role weighting, expansion/compression basis, stopping point, provenance, omissions, corrections, and detector evidence separately.

## Developmental architecture and implementation split — P0/P3

For a substantial whole-article review, use the smallest justified role separation:

1. **Developmental reviewer (P0):** diagnose article promise, destination, reader stakes, distinctiveness, credibility timing, heading hierarchy, recurrence by function, section autonomy, pacing, appendix division, and ending function. Do not rewrite.
2. **Controlled implementer (P3):** apply only accepted recommendations through moves, trims, pointers, hierarchy repairs, and the minimum necessary connective prose. Preserve the authority register and edit contract.
3. **Owner decision:** record Joel's acceptance, rejection, qualification, or rewrite. A rejected operation may still preserve a valid diagnosis.
4. **Independent review (P0):** inspect the complete implemented article for whether the diagnosed problem was solved without flattening the route, voice, evidence, or controversy.
5. **Detector:** run only after fidelity, coherence, article function, and editorial quality pass.

Before global recommendations, record the operational article purpose: mystery/problem, destination, necessary route, and payoff. A suspenseful or thriller-shaped article may delay the answer, but not the stakes. Audit what only Joel has, where the skeptical reader spends trust, whether the heading tree represents the real argument, and what action the ending must perform.

When evidence is correct but badly timed, use **point and move**: preserve the firm early claim, add the smallest forward pointer, move the detailed evidence to the later decision point, and verify that the destination now carries the complete support. Do not manufacture uncertainty merely to solve a sequencing problem.

## Layered doctrinal, scientific, or interpretive correction — P0/P2S/P3

- separate primary source, lived experience, established theory, Joel's synthesis, proposed mechanism, exercise rationale, analogy, external evidence, competing interpretation, and uncertainty;
- record source access as full text, abstract, snippet, or secondhand; never invent Joel's personal source reaction;
- map each source to all of its real roles, including support and countermodel; do not let broad source scope license a broader thesis;
- preserve evidence-stream independence and whether experience preceded reading;
- keep common access, binding, unity, reflective knowing, self-ascription, appropriation, and renewed becoming distinct;
- for source-language claims, search formulas, grammar, synonyms, translations, and parallels; distinguish lexical gaps from conceptual incapacity;
- treat negative textual claims as provisional until neighboring formula families are audited;
- scope direct episodes or meditation states so they are not mistaken for permanent functioning;
- preserve the exact modifier identifying what ceases, is grasped, or is released;
- identify the superseded judgment and literal/nonliteral equivalents across title, opening, summaries, headings, glossary, appendix, diagrams, analogies, captions, conclusion, links, and derivatives;
- preserve independently supported parts of the broader model and use progressive disclosure between main article and appendix;
- in P0, report exact review targets only; in authorized P2S/P3, repair every dependency and run the orphan audit.
