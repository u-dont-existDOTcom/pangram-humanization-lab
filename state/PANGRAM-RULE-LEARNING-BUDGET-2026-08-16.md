## Stopping rule

The 20 calls are permission, not a target. Stop before 20 when:

- the candidate rule is already discriminated from its main alternatives;
- remaining tests would be token/phrase hunting;
- the effect is distributed and further subdivision would not produce an editorially usable rule;
- the hypothesis fails, reverses, or becomes too confounded to justify more paid evidence;
- a cross-boundary replication shows the effect is too local to promote.

Use additional calls preferentially for **cross-boundary replication, interaction tests, exact repeats of surprising effects, and holdouts**, rather than finer lexical subdivision of a passage that is already understood.
