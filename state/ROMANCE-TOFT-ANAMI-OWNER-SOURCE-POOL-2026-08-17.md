- The claim “only ~20% of women have the desirable physiology that tightens the vaginal walls in response to stimulation” was **not substantiated** in targeted PubMed searches. Do not use the number without its source.
- Vaginal/pelvic-floor physiology is variable and more complex: vaginal distension/stimulation can evoke levator-ani reflex contraction; orgasmic vaginal/pelvic contractions are common but not universal; pelvic-floor training can improve strength and sexual function in some women. These facts do not establish a fixed 20% responder phenotype.
