## 3. Conversational prose should preserve the connective a speaker would actually say

Joel identified another assistant construction as strongly AI-like:

> Meditation sometimes made the deprivation easier. The deprivation stayed.

The problem is not merely sentence length. The second hard stop hides the actual spoken relation between the thoughts and produces a dry, cheeky, artificially compressed cadence. In ordinary conversation, the speaker would usually keep the relation audible with `but`, `and`, `so`, `because`, `even though`, or another natural connective.

Joel's preferred repair was:

> Meditation sometimes helped me cope better with the deprivation, but it wasn't a cure.

Durable Joel-byline rule: when prose is meant to sound conversational, read it as speech. If a normal speaker would naturally join two thoughts with a conjunction, do not split them merely for punch, compression, or pseudo-cleverness. This is not a ban on short sentences or fragments; a fragment can remain when a person would actually say it that way.

## 4. Skew toward active voice when that is the natural spoken form

Joel also flagged `the deprivation stayed` as passive/abstract in effect: the sentence turns an experienced condition into a detached object rather than keeping the person experiencing it in view.

Default toward active or experiencer-centered phrasing when it sounds natural: `I still felt deprived`, `it still hurt`, `I kept wanting...`, etc. Do not mechanically rewrite every grammatical passive. The test is whether the alternative sounds more like somebody actually talking and preserves the intended actor/experience.

## 5. Audit conspicuous local word repetition

In conversational prose, repeated content words can become conspicuous even when each sentence is individually fine. Joel flagged three uses of `most` in three sentences in the celibacy paragraph.

Do a local repetition pass during cold audit. Vary syntax or combine sentences when the repetition is accidental. Preserve deliberate repetition when it has an actual rhetorical function; do not replace words with strained synonyms merely to achieve lexical variety.

## 6. Conversational does not mean lazy or unstructured

The target is natural spoken logic with edited clarity: sentences a person could plausibly say aloud, while retaining organization, precision, rhythm, and argument. Do not simulate speech with filler, rambling, fake hesitations, or arbitrary fragments. The governing question is whether the prose sounds like a thoughtful person speaking naturally rather than a model performing a literary conversational style.
