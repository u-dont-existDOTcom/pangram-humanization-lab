## Two substantive improvements to the evidence

First, evaluation chronology is not revision ancestry. Five comparisons require reversing the timestamp order because an older source was rerun after its edited variant. The manifest names the actual before/after relation instead of mechanically pairing consecutive test calls.

Second, fidelity and detector progress are separate. Four of the seven categorical detector gains in the 30 comparisons come from pairs classified as content-changing or alignment-uncertain. That does not mean all four edits are bad; it means their gains cannot be used as clean evidence about style alone. Authorized article cuts remain authorized; this audit does not revert them.

## A useful new teaching candidate: forgiveness

Earlier ending:

> The harm happened, and the boundary still matters. The person is still accountable, and forgiveness may come later on its own.

Later ending:

> They really did hurt us and they still have to answer for it, and our boundary with them still matters, but one day we may find that we have forgiven them.

The earlier complete paragraph has AI-labeled ending segments; the later saved paragraph is Human/High. Harm, accountability, boundaries and possible later forgiveness remain. My reading is that the revision lets the clauses relate through people and an unfolding recognition instead of presenting four abstract, evenly paired propositions. That interpretation is provisional and several realization features changed together. This is a candidate teaching episode, not a universal instruction to make longer sentences.

The percentages example does the opposite at the surface: it separates one packed sentence into several shorter sentences while retaining its explanation, two values and range qualification. Both outcomes argue against treating sentence splitting or joining as a universally sufficient operation; they do not by themselves establish the shared causal mechanism.

## What is frozen and what is not

PILOT-RULES.json fixes the proposed comparison rules: identical source constraints and budget, current method versus current method plus a few matched literal teaching episodes, one first attempt per arm, genuinely separate contexts, and separate fidelity/naturalness/detector outcomes. It is a whole-method comparison, not a disguised one-feature test.

The **30-target prospective cohort has not been assembled or tested**. This 20-family familiar archive is development material. Selecting 30 contrasts from it would not create 30 independent test cases. The next work is to recover more clean source-aligned examples and construct a new-family target manifest before paid measurement. No API calls, model-weight training, independent fresh evaluation, article edits, or publication actions occurred in this pass.
