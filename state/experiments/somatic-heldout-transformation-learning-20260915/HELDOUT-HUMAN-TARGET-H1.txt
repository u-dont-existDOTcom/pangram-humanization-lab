Brainspotting is a lot more accessible for most people than water therapy. You don’t need a pool at home, and you don’t even need a therapist in many cases for this. It’s another type of gentle exploration. It doesn’t go so deep and feel so amazing as water therapy of course, but it does help to find where, in your actual field of vision, do you feel safe vs re-activated.
