

How I Know Whether It Actually Helped

My outcome measure is boring: does the thing still hit as hard later? By restimulation I mean what happens when I remember the event again. Does the same charge come back into the body and mind, or not?

While I was working on this article I was still upset about an argument with my housemate. I had been washing her moldy clothes for her, one of the dresses got discolored, and she yelled at me about it. I was still stewing over how she treated me.

So I did a quick bit of self-Brainspotting. I moved my finger and eyes slowly around while thinking about the event. When my gaze went farther to either side I noticed blissful tingles as I moved into more peripheral vision. I do not take that as proof of the simple claim that “peripheral vision activates the parasympathetic nervous system”; the research is more complicated than that.

Subjectively, I could move from the more settled side with the blissful tingles to the distressed side from moment to moment and kind of move the bliss into the distress while I was thinking about the event. Afterward it felt as though the event held less negative energy, or less blocking energy, in my mind.

I check again an hour later. If I'm still stewing just as hard, it probably didn't last, and I may need to work with it again. If I can remember the event and it doesn't grab me as hard, now that's interesting. I don't expect one pass to erase every trigger; EMDR often takes more than one session too.
