Complicated explanations can be impressive without being very useful. Ask why something works and you can get ten mechanisms, six acronyms, and a beautiful diagram. That's fine if the mechanisms matter, but sometimes the only question you actually needed answered was whether the thing works well enough to bother with. Researchers get trapped in this too. People are strangely loyal to ideas they came up with themselves. If somebody else gives you a bad idea, it's pretty easy to throw it away. If you spent three weeks researching your own bad idea, suddenly abandoning it feels like wasting three weeks. But the time is already gone either way.
