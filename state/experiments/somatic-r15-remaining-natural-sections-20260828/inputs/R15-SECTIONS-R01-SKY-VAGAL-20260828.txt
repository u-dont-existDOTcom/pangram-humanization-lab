Sky Hypnosis and Vagal Blitz

Sky Hypnosis and Vagal Blitz are different animals from ordinary regulation practices. They are high-intensity state-shifters, and a stable person may find them useful even before doing any of the therapies above. They are not prerequisites for healing.

Deep memory work, immersive child dialogue, deep hypnosis, and suggestive exploration are things I save for when I can stay present, stop voluntarily, and come back afterward.

Sky Hypnosis can make you lightheaded. Vagal Blitz is a lying-down-only practice with cardiovascular and mental-health cautions. I would use the complete guide, start with the least intense version that gives a useful shift, and stop when balance, orientation, or recovery becomes unreliable. I do not chase fainting or loss of consciousness.

I also do not count “wow” as an outcome measure. A huge release, a lot of bliss, or an altered-state shift can feel profound and still leave the actual problem basically untouched. The test is the same one I use everywhere else: when I remember the event later, am I actually less restimulated? Am I more able to handle pain, boundaries, conflict, and ordinary life? If the practice is mainly helping me avoid grief, conflict, practical problems, or deeper relational healing, the extraordinary state does not rescue it.
