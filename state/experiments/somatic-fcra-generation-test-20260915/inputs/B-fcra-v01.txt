When activation runs away with you, Somatic Experiencing gives you something smaller to work with than the whole trauma story. You can stay with a sensation, an impulse, orientation, or a defensive response, with the pacing set by how much is actually manageable. Titration, pendulation, and orientation are different ways of touching something difficult in a small dose and then coming back. That is the practical side of the “window of tolerance” idea. If you can already do that and you have one clear target, you may be ready to work on the trauma itself; preparation should not become the treatment forever.
