## Hypotheses

The current high-information candidates are not the conspicuous colloquialisms themselves. They are deeper realization properties shared by the two paragraphs.

### H1 — contextual anchoring / referential dependence

The paragraphs are not fully standalone mini-essays. P66 is introduced as `this part of the map` and refers backward to `prior therapies`; P17 begins with `Those therapies`. This may reduce generic editorial self-containment.

Prediction: removing article-local anchoring while preserving the claims may increase AI risk. A Human result would make contextual anchoring insufficient by itself.

### H2a — active process realization versus abstract process packaging (P66)

P66 realizes reconsolidation through verbs/actions rather than a compact nominal definition. The test converts only that process phrase into a more nominalized package while retaining the surrounding stripped control.

Prediction: nominalization may increase risk if the active process realization is carrying Human shape. A Human result makes this factor insufficient by itself.

### H2b — heterogeneous / lopsided problem realization versus matched branches (P17)

P17's two stated problems are not realized as matched textbook units: the first is source/evidence/reporting-heavy; the second is mechanistic and conceptual. The test keeps both claims but regularizes them into matched `First` / `Second` two-sentence branches.

Prediction: matched branch realization may increase risk if the original lopsidedness is protective. A Human result makes this factor insufficient by itself.
