This is the part of the map called targeted memory work or “reconsolidation.” The term refers to the way we process the charged memory, reconsider its significance, and integrate it in a healthier way while still being honest about what happened. The approach works best for flashbacks to a specific event rather than something more general that brainspotting or other prior therapies could address less specifically. Its goal is to reduce the persistence of these memories so they don’t keep interfering when they return.
