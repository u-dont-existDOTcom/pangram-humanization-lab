# Human→AI minimal-pair lessons — 2026-08-28

Status: **COMPLETE / durable detector research / not article authority**

The useful causal unit is an editorial operation **in its exact boundary and combination**, not a suspicious word or sentence in isolation.

## Findings by register

### Confessional owner prose

On the R08 adult/child-trust baseline, explanatory completion stayed Human, sentence equalization stayed Human, and their combination stayed Human. Neither polished cadence nor one balanced interpretive sentence was sufficient to create an AI classification in this register.

### Long research-conversational owner prose

One closed three-part taxonomy stayed Human. One abstract method/recommendations bridge stayed Human. Together they became Mixed at AI `0.132098034`, with Pangram placing the signal in the middle.

Replacing only the bridge with a first-person colloquial version restored Human. Keeping the bridge but replacing only the neat taxonomy with two uneven colloquial sentences also restored Human. The local risk was therefore not `three items` or `a transition` by itself; it was the adjacent package of polished bridge + closed parallel taxonomy.

### Compact Somatic explanation

The following stayed Human:

- paragraph packing alone;
- explicit cross-domain relation in colloquial wording;
- the full compressed realization without explicit cross-domain synthesis;
- compression confined to the explanatory middle paragraph.

The polished transition alone made the baseline Mixed at AI `0.3076923192`, with later-part signal. Compression plus polished abstract synthesis was AI `1.0`. That full-AI interaction reproduced when `The same limit appears...` was replaced by the independently tested `The same principle extends...` realization. This is strong evidence for a boundary-level **abstract compression × polished cross-domain synthesis** interaction rather than a single magic phrase.

## Production implications

1. Preserve strong owner source and use minimum normalization where it already performs the thought. R08 shows that publication cleanup can remain Human.
2. Treat model-written connective tissue as a real intervention. Ask whether it advances time, case, premise, consequence, or the live question; remove it when it only announces the relationship the reader can already see.
3. Do not stack a neat taxonomy, polished bridge, balanced contrast, and compressed synthesis merely because each seems harmless alone. Adjacent editorial closures can interact.
4. When protected functions must be added, distribute them through necessary concrete moves anchored in owner cognition or ordinary action. Avoid packing every function into one compact explanatory paragraph with named roles, a fallback taxonomy, a coined label, action, and caveat.
5. Do not create phrase blacklists. The colloquial counterexamples and null cells show that topology, register, compression, and neighboring operations matter.
6. Short-text Pangram results carried Pangram's own limited-confidence warning. Use them for controlled local comparison, never authorship proof or editorial authority.

R09 is the motivating production example: its byte-identical R08 paragraph remained Human/high in owner localization, while the appended 66-word model/editorial paragraph was AI/high. The experiment explains why that second paragraph is risky as a package; it does not authorize deleting its protected functions or replacing owner-final prose.
