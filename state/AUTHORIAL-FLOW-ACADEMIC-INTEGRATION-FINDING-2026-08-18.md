## Finding

Academic writing-process research independently supports testing the project's recurrent-composition hypothesis: emerging text can participate in generating subsequent content, and discourse/process structure can carry writer-specific signal. The same literature also limits the claim: deliberate rhetorical planning is a legitimate human mode, pauses/revisions are not direct cognition labels, and observed process features can be task/register dependent.

The resulting project architecture therefore keeps three planes separate: product/semantic flow, observable composition trace, and task-relevant authorial-state hypothesis. The next experimental condition is recurrent source-gated live selection (RSG-LS), with controller selection, writer generation, fidelity, and flow kept as separate roles.

This remains provisional until RSG-LS reduces source-to-sentence serialization and premature completion without fidelity loss across multiple source packets/registers, and until any personalized process signal is validated on held-out task-matched data.
