So I reread the draft and picture the kid who wrote that first angry sentence still gripping the pencil, because he is hurt and at least the sentence is doing something about it. But then you read it once more and pause over one phrase that seems almost pleased with how much it might sting, though maybe that isn’t quite fair either, and the adult is still there, waiting, not quite sure what to keep.

Advanced AI detection tools don’t treat a document as a single giant block of text. Rather, these tools break down the text into segments, sentences, and paragraphs.
