When I go back to the part of me who wrote that reply, what do I expect to find—someone cruel, someone I need to correct before he speaks again? I look closer and notice how tightly he is holding on to each sentence, as if letting one go would mean losing the thing beneath it. Is he asking me to defend the wording? No. He is, though; not because the words were fair, but because they were the only proof he had that something in him objected. What happens if I don’t take that proof away? His anger does not disappear, as I thought it might. It shifts just enough for me to hear the hurt inside it asking whether I am here to edit him or stay.
