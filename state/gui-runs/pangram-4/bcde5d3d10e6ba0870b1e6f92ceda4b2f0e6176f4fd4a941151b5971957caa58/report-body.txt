Of course, deal with anything unsafe or practical first.
Say last time you went back into the wound, something genuinely changed. Maybe you finally felt what you'd only been explaining to yourself; maybe afterward you made a different choice. I don't want to call that checking just because you've been over the same ground before. The harder case is when the main thing that changed is... nothing, really; you still don't feel sure, so you ask again whether you've finally grieved it correctly. And then again. After a hundred rounds of that, I start wondering whether healing has become part of the hook. The wound hasn't suddenly become fake because of that. Unfortunately grief repeats itself too, so the question coming back isn't enough to tell you whether the child has something new to say.
At some point you may have to leave that unresolved and go back to your day. If the question comes with you, okay. You don't have to hold another meeting just because your mind wants one.
If these exercises start feeling like a ritual you have to complete before you're allowed to live, stop there.
