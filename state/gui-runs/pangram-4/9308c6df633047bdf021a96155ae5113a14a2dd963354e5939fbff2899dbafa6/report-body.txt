When to Change the Strategy
Weeks in and nothing much is changing? I’d want to quit too, or else keep doing the exact same thing forever so I never have to find out what's underneath this. Before going with either of those paths, look for what exactly is in the way. Sometimes the strategy was not actually implemented, just read about or played around with lightly. Or there wasn't the right environment to really practice it, like no alone time. Or perhaps you did sit down with your little one, but it felt scary or like some part of you was trying to protect you from going deeper. Whatever it turns out to be, it makes sense to work on that blocker instead of giving up on everything.

Maybe you've been trying to offer your little one comfort for weeks, and the words are all there, but the loving grown-up behind them isn't there yet.
 Some people keep trying sweeter and sweeter things, then throw the baby out with the bathwater when that doesn't help and decide reparenting is useless. I'd borrow that grown-up first, try again, and see if your little one can tell the difference.

Say an exercise leaves you feeling lighter for a few hours, but then the old feeling comes back later on.
 Some relief is nice, but we still want to know if we are fixing the problem at a deeper level. If you feel like keeping track, jot down what changed and how long it lasted.
 If that feeds into your OCD patterns, drop it.

Sometimes a session goes great, and a few days later you’re skipping lunch and saying yes to everything again. I wouldn’t try going deeper next time. I’d guess you just haven’t brought any of it out with you yet. Whatever you did for your little one in there, try a tiny bit of it out here, like texting a friend that you’re having a rough day instead of saying you’re fine.

How many tries would I give it?
 It's not like we can just put a number on it.
 Some things shift in a day. Others take about as long as learning guitar.
 But do notice if it continues feeling about the same each time, or if it's giving indicators of greater and greater improvement. If it’s making things worse, or you just can’t do it right now, I’d change course sooner (stopping counts). Not wanting to do it anymore is a reason too, but if that “nope” sounds like a part of you that’s trying to protect you, I’d hear it out first.
 If it still says no, that’s your answer.
 And if you keep following the reparenting steps correctly without getting the expected results, consider if you have a unique case these steps don’t match yet. Maybe you’ll find something that works better for you, or you can ask me what I’d do for this case, or ask a therapist you work with. Either way, I’d like to hear about it!
