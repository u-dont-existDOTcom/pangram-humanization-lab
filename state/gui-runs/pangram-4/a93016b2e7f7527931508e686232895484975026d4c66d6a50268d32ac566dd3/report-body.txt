What does the percentage mean?
–

Advanced AI detection tools don’t treat a document as a single giant block of text. Rather, these tools break down the text into segments, sentences, and paragraphs. These individual chunks are scored as segments. If you get a 30% score on a 10-page paper, it likely means that roughly three pages worth of text display the patterns that are typical of an LLM. If you are using an AI detector and a particular document gets a low score (e.g., 30% AI-generated), this usually indicates a hybrid document.
