After I revised the message, I went back to the one who had written the ugly version and said, “See? This says it better.” He heard: You ruin everything when you speak. Beneath the accusations was something I had missed because I disliked the sound of it: “That wasn’t okay.” The words around it were reckless, but the protest itself wasn’t. “You were trying to say no,” I said, and this time he didn’t answer; he just stopped bracing as if I were about to take the sentence away.
