There's a point where even inner work can start acting like the hook. Something really did hurt, and maybe your response to it wasn't great either. So you go back in: was that grief, or fear, or old stuff? Am I processing this correctly? Did I stop too soon? At first you might actually learn something. But if you're circling the same question because you need to feel completely sure before you can go back to dinner or answer a friend, I'm not convinced another round is automatically more caring. The hurt can be real and the checking can still be keeping you stuck.
