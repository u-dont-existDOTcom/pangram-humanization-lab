This happens to me all the time with texts. I'll be writing a reply and, if I'm doing well, catch myself halfway through and think, wait, that's not actually the best way to put this. I'm not trying to hide that I'm upset. I'm trying to say the same thing in a way the other person has a chance of hearing. Text makes this much easier than talking, obviously, because you get another pass before you send it, and I think that's good practice for speaking too. Sometimes the rewrite is much shorter; sometimes I realize there's nothing useful to send. Owning what I did wrong is trickier when what I did wrong was mostly just not communicating with very high IQ and EQ. If I actually did something I know was wrong, that's easier for me. Being too direct is harder to apologize for, because at some level I still associate directness with honesty.
