When I reread the ugly first draft, I try not to treat the writer of it like some embarrassing little man who had to be removed before the adults could speak. He may be angry, repetitive, unfair in places, whatever, but angry and messy aren’t the same thing as childish, and underneath all that he was usually trying to get one thing understood. But then I think maybe “understood” isn’t even quite it. Sometimes he wants to know somebody is going to protect him, or tell him he isn’t crazy, or help with whatever just happened, and fixing the message so another person can hear it doesn’t do that part. That’s a different conversation, the one I have to go back and have with him after I’ve made the reply presentable, and I don’t always know yet what he’s asking me for.
