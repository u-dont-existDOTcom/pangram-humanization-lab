AI Highlight
When you open the draft again, there’s an awkward second when you can’t quite believe you nearly sent it. The swollen phrases sit there trying too hard, and one sentence has the unmistakable sound of a door being slammed in writing. You may want to clean the whole thing up fast, partly to spare yourself the embarrassment of having sounded so angry. But then you read one of the worst lines again and notice that, underneath its clumsy accusation, it is objecting to something real: no, that wasn’t fair; no, you don’t get to speak to me that way and then act as if nothing happened. The wording is bad, but the protest isn’t. That changes the job a little. The more adult part of you can take over the reply without turning on the person who wrote it, can remove what would only start another fight while leaving some trace of what he was trying to protect (even if he made a mess of saying it). You edit a sentence, put one back, soften another, and still hover over the ugliest line longer than you expected, because it’s the first place in the draft where you can hear that he meant no.

If you are using an AI detector and a particular document gets a low score (e.g., 30% AI-generated), this usually indicates a hybrid document.
