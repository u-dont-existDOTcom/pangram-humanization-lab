## R1 — Talk cumulative-overcompletion holdout

Boundary: Joel's direct owner rewrite of `Talk about making love before you do it`.

Variants:
1. exact owner baseline;
2. owner baseline + one appended interpretive/summary sentence;
3. owner baseline + three appended aftercare/summary moves.

All three variants were Pangram 4.0 Human with AI fraction 0.0. The owner baseline window had AI-assistance score ~0.00023; +one ~0.0040. The three-sentence package also remained Human.

### Interpretation

The Vows result does **not** justify a universal rule that stacking several post-landing sentences automatically causes Pangram to flag a passage. Cumulative overcompletion is a real editorial/function concept and was detector-causal in the controlled Vows ending, but the detector effect did not replicate on this Talk boundary.

This strengthens the distinction between:
- editorial overcompletion as a functional prose defect; and
- Pangram sensitivity to a particular overcompletion package on a particular boundary.

Do not use Pangram as the reason to cut aftercare. Cut it when the thought has already landed or the sentence has no live reader job.

## R2 — Primal source-reporting holdout

Boundary: `The Queen`, 218–220 words.

Controlled contrast:
- formal/source-certifying realization: `That laboratory evidence establishes the uniqueness of the phenomenon.`
- plain realization: `That's what shows the phenomenon is unique.`

Both were Pangram 4.0 Human with AI fraction 0.0. Assistance scores were ~0.0418 formal vs ~0.0155 plain.

### Interpretation

The strong Vows Bible-boundary regression from formal source-certification language did not replicate as a classification flip here. The plain version scored somewhat more human-like continuously, but both were comfortably Human.

Promote only the editorial rule: when a plain source relation carries the claim accurately, prefer it over unnecessary evidence-processing/reporting scaffolding. Do **not** promote a detector rule that formal attribution is intrinsically AI-like.

## R3 — Primal idiolect factorial

- genericized: `some critics dismiss this topic as fiction`

All four cells were Pangram 4.0 Human with AI fraction 0.0. Recorded assistance scores include ~0.0155 owner/owner, ~0.0263 generic-life/owner-critic, and ~0.0474 owner-life/generic-critic; the fully genericized cell also remained Human.

### Interpretation

The dramatic Vows `y'all` near-threshold flip does not generalize into a simple rule that distinctive owner idiolect is required for Human classification. On a comfortably Human boundary, removing two conspicuous owner-specific clusters did not change the class.

The Vows result is therefore better modeled as a **near-threshold boundary interaction** than as evidence for lexical seasoning. Distinctive owner language remains valuable because it is Joel's voice, not because we should sprinkle idiolect to manipulate Pangram.

## Cross-boundary conclusions

1. **Cumulative overcompletion is not a universal detector trigger.** It was causally important in Vows and null in Talk. Preserve the editorial stopping-point rule independently of detector behavior.
2. **Formal source-certification sensitivity is boundary-local.** Vows showed a localized regression; Primal did not. Prefer natural source relations for prose quality and fidelity, not as a Pangram blacklist.
3. **Owner idiolect is not a magic detector ingredient.** Genericizing two conspicuous owner clusters on Primal stayed Human in every factorial cell.
4. **Near-threshold interaction is the strongest surviving explanation of the Vows `y'all` flip.** Pangram can resegment or reinterpret a large boundary after a tiny local change when the surrounding passage is already near its decision surface.
5. **Controlled nulls are valuable.** The cross-boundary failures prevent local detector effects from hardening into global writing rules.
6. For production writing, semantic sanity, lived reality, curious-reader sequence, provenance, and real stopping point remain the operative rules. Detector evidence should diagnose a specific boundary only after those are satisfied.
