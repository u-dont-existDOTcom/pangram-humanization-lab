## Supported interpretation

1. **Historical detector windows are localization clues, not stable rewrite truth.** The current natural section can relocate or eliminate the apparent failure.
2. **A heading is not automatically a valid detector or authorial unit.** Some prose receives its authorial function from an adjacent lived sequence; forcing every heading to become a self-contained mini-essay can itself create model shape.
3. **Overcompletion is a structural failure, not merely verbosity.** Expanding one governing question into a comprehensive advice curriculum can erase the specific lived pressure that generated the advice.
4. **After two faithful rewrites preserve the same failure, challenge the container/job before writing version three.** Synonym-level or sentence-level variation is unlikely to repair a section-level architecture problem.
5. **Concrete examples can perform conclusions.** In the Casual Sex section, deleting abstract aftercare and allowing the lived college-friend example to carry the fulfillment claim improved the entire section to 100% Human.
