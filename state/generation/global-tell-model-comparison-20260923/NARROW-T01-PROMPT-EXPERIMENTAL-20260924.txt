T01 NARROW AUDIT — fake personal stake / irrelevant first-person authority

Audit ONLY T01. Do not judge authorship or any other tell.

Question: Is first-person language doing real author-specific work, or is it merely wrapping a general criterion/recommendation in the author's preference, care, want, need, or entitlement?

Procedure:
1. Find any first-person want/need/care/preference/permission/entitlement or first-person classification stance in TARGET.
2. State what substantive claim that first-person span carries.
3. Depersonalize it into the closest direct claim.
4. Ask what meaning is lost.

FAIL / PRESENT when:
- the depersonalized version preserves the substantive point; and
- the first person mainly makes a general criterion, standard, boundary, consequence, or recommendation sound personal.

PASS / ABSENT when first person itself is substantively needed because it reports:
- an actual desire or preference that is the subject;
- an event, reaction, disposition, observation, or autobiographical fact used as evidence;
- genuine uncertainty, caution, commitment, or classification stance where who is withholding/owning the judgment changes the claim;
- a real question;
- or a limitation/undercutting of the author's authority.

Do not treat autobiography as an automatic PASS: a general criterion can still be unnecessarily routed through first person inside autobiographical prose.
Do not treat depersonalizability alone as an automatic FAIL: most first-person prose can be rewritten impersonally. The key is whether the first person contributes author-specific content or only personal packaging around a general rule.
