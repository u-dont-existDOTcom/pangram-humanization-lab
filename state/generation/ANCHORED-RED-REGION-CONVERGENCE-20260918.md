# Anchored red-region convergence under constant Human context

Date: 2026-09-18
Status: **VALIDATED ON ONE INNER CHILD SUBSECTION / ACTIVE METHOD HYPOTHESIS**

## Validated pattern

A difficult subsection converged when the tested natural boundary had:

`AI red opening -> Human/high middle -> AI red ending`

The Human/high middle was frozen exactly.

Only the red flanks were regenerated.

The complete boundary was remeasured after each local repair.

Measured sequence:
- F: AI 0.5329530835
- G: AI 0.4243027866
- H: AI 0.0 / Human 1.0

The middle remained constant throughout.

## Key distinction

This is not equivalent to giving a writer an external Human style sample.

A separate calibration sample may fail to transfer.

Here the Human prose is **inside the actual candidate boundary** and remains unchanged while neighboring model prose changes.

That creates:
- a stable detector context;
- earned edit scope;
- comparable measurements across attempts;
- protection against destroying known-good prose.

## Generation operations that accompanied convergence

F -> G:
- keep green interior;
- move red opening from explicit diagnosis/criterion language toward chronology/social observation;
- simplify red ending from explanatory loop toward a shorter interaction process.

G -> H:
- keep green interior;
- change red opening's pragmatic act to a source-grounded reader-recognition question;
- use ordinary social reaction and uneven sentence duration;
- change red ending to direct attention/question rather than explanation;
- retain source meaning with rougher ordinary diction and less counselor aftercare.

## Contrast case

The adjacent Write It. Don't Send It Yet subsection had no known Human interior anchor.

Six materially varied all-model boundaries all returned AI 1.0.

This does not prove the missing anchor caused failure, but it makes the anchor hypothesis decision-relevant.

## Next test

A strong discriminating test is to create one semantically native owner-written Human core inside the failing subsection, then repeat the anchored red-region loop.

Do not misreport success from such a test as autonomous all-model humanization.

It would establish contextual model humanization around a Human anchor.
