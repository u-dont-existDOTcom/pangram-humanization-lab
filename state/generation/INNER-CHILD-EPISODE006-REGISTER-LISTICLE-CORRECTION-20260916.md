## Refined interpretation

The named-role-model hypothesis is falsified by this contrast. The Human/high version preserves the same semantic alternatives without adding any named person. What changes is how the thought is spoken:

- AI-shaped realization compresses the alternatives into one abstract categorical string;
- Human realization breaks the same thought into ordinary spoken sentences and uses plainer register (`learn a bit of adulting` rather than `experience one reliable adult function`).

The outer five-stage numbered list remains unchanged. The stage-4 conceptual triad also remains unchanged. This strengthens the conclusion that the **internal listicle/taxonomy packaging of stage 2** was the decisive local weakness in this contrast.

## Causal limit

Do not reduce this to a magic phrase or universal rule. Topology and register change together, so the evidence does not isolate which of those two variables carries how much detector weight. It does, however, sharply rule out the need for named human referents and shows that the same semantic alternatives can survive in a Human/high realization.

## Production lesson

When a legitimate list/map contains an abstract category string, first try saying the same thing in ordinary speech rather than adding anecdotes or dismantling the list. Speak closer to the reader's lived state and farther from academic/therapist taxonomy while preserving meaning.
