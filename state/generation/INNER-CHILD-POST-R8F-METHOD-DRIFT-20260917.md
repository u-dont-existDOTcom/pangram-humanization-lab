## General lesson

When a recent process has direct success evidence, **check for method drift before escalating to a new strategy**.

A valid audit sequence is:

1. recover the last successful method fingerprint;
2. compare the failed run dimension-by-dimension against it;
3. identify drift versus faithful-method failure;
4. restore the last validated method when the failure came from drift;
5. escalate strategy only after the validated method itself fails under comparable conditions or new evidence undermines its causal premise.
