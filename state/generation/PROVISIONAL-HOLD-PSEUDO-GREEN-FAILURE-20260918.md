# Provisional hold is not green evidence — Episode 008 Red1

Date: 2026-09-18
Status: **PROJECT-SPECIFIC METHOD CORRECTION / ACTIVE CALIBRATION**

## Trigger

During Inner Child Therapy Red1 repair, the supervisor marked a model paragraph as `PROVISIONALLY HOLD` because it looked materially better than the previous fresh-writer attempt.

The paragraph had:
- no owner endorsement;
- no detector localization;
- no genuinely independent fresh-reader validation.

Later fresh paragraph-2 work treated that paragraph as fixed while repeatedly repairing the tail.

A stricter whole-boundary audit then found that the held paragraph still:
- participated in a polished mini-essay staircase;
- contained an unsupported Joel-personal-preference wrapper;
- used stronger-than-needed rhetorical intensifiers.

## Failure mechanism

The label `provisionally hold` was intended as an experiment convenience.

Operationally, it behaved like a green lock:
- the region stopped receiving structural scrutiny;
- the edit scope narrowed around adjacent text;
- later iterations optimized the tail around an unvalidated model region.

This created a **pseudo-green**.

## Production rule

In coarse-to-fine humanization:

- `hold for one experiment` is not equivalent to `green`;
- same-context relative improvement is not enough to freeze a model-written span;
- a span may be frozen as a reliable Human/good island only when supported by the current task's admissible external evidence, such as owner judgment, detector localization where useful, or another genuinely independent evidence source.

If a temporarily held span has no such evidence, keep its status explicit:
`UNVALIDATED_HOLD`.

Do not let an unvalidated hold silently narrow the campaign into residual repair.

## Relationship to Episode 007

The successful Episode 007 campaign narrowed only after stable Human/high islands existed from external localization.

The lesson is not that detector evidence is always mandatory. The lesson is that **edit scope should narrow in response to reliable good-region evidence, not merely because the current critic prefers one model paragraph over another**.

Do not treat this as a universal ban on temporary experimental holds. The correction is about preventing a temporary hold from acquiring green-lock semantics without evidence.
