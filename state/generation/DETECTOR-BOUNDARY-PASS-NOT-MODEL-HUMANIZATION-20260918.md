# Detector-boundary pass is not model-humanization proof

Date: 2026-09-18
Status: **ACTIVE OWNER-CALIBRATED METHOD RULE**

## Trigger

A mixed Inner Child subsection containing:
- a 113-word owner-written Human/medium paragraph;
- model-written prose before and after it;

was rearranged until Pangram 4.0 labeled the complete boundary Human 1.0.

Joel then directly corrected the interpretation:

the model-written parts still read AI to him, and Pangram also reads those model parts as AI outside the mixed boundary.

## Rule

Do not equate:

`complete mixed boundary -> Pangram Human`

with:

`model-written prose -> humanized`.

A known-Human block can alter contextual segmentation/scoring enough to mask adjacent AI-looking model prose.

## Required evidence planes

For model-humanization claims, keep separate:

1. **Owner/editorial judgment of the model-written span itself**
2. **Detector behavior on the model-written span / a boundary not dominated by owner Human prose**
3. **Full integrated-boundary detector behavior**
4. **Preservation/fidelity**

The strongest humanization claim requires all relevant planes to agree.

## Historical consequence

Detector-Human model examples that Joel later says still look AI are detector-transfer/boundary evidence only, not positive style targets.

This already applies to:
- EFT successful model transfer;
- `The same principle extends...`;
- `This also happens...`;
- explanatory completion controls.

Episode 007 My Journey R8F is stronger because Joel explicitly accepted the final boundary as `perfect, 100% human high conf`, but its one changed model sentence was not independently detector-certified in isolation.

## Production consequence

Do not use Human anchors as detector shields.

Use owner Human prose as:
- article authority where appropriate;
- teaching/cognition evidence;
- contextual calibration.

But a model flank remains failed if it still reads AI to Joel, regardless of the whole-boundary Pangram label.

This protects the workflow against detector-version upgrades that remove current context sensitivity.
