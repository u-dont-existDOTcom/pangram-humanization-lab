## What changed, with mechanism

1. The opener no longer merely announces available options, then walks through the author's narrated hypothetical actions. **"But if you know ... destabilize you, explore ..."** puts the consequence of the reader's actual vulnerability immediately into a concrete decision. The risk condition is retained.
2. **"Metta would be my starting point"** grounds first person in the author's real judgment rather than repeating mechanical **"I’d start ... If ... I still wanted ... If I still wanted ..."** instructions. It does not require an absolute ban on "I'd"; the question is **why does the author's wish matter to this reader**.
3. Rather than generic *wanting more*, the new passage gives the reader a reason for further practice: **"gaining more insight or self-awareness"**. This is new author cognition, not recoverable from the original purely as a stylistic change. It makes the conditional progression psychologically motivated.
4. The author adds a balanced practical thought—**a retreat can provide a nice practice environment if set up well**—so the next question about safety isn't simply a formulaic disclaimer attached to a hazardous option.
5. The reader now asks the retreat **what happens if destabilization starts, and whether pausing is an acceptable option there**; institutional practice remains the decision-changing variable. First person is retained only where it carries the author's actual preference.
6. The sentence movement is *reason for gentler exploration → conditional deeper practice goal → conditional benefit of retreat → specific question for center*, not a repeated narrator-first if/then safety checklist.

## Reusable positive lesson, narrowly stated
