# Inner Child Episode 008 — R11 owner-lock + remainder regression

Status: PROVISIONAL METHOD EVIDENCE
Date: 2026-09-16
Branch: `task/humanization-method-fingerprint-20260916`

## Observed evidence

The owner-final three-paragraph `Catch the Hook` realization was owner-reported Human/high.

A later R11 candidate preserved that three-paragraph block exactly and appended assistant-written prose to restore source-required functions not explicit in the owner endpoint: Guide/direction, ten-minute harm reduction, send/consume/promise/decide delay, literal words-at-time versus later verbal translation, and stop/external-support safety aftercare.

Owner reports the complete R11 subsection as **100% AI / high confidence**.

Immediately before the owner repair ladder, three different model-predicted realizations of the subsection were each owner-reported **AI / medium confidence**.

## Diagnosis

### Primary: semantic-completion architecture was reintroduced

The owner Human/high endpoint did not explicitly close every semantic obligation in the local subsection. R11 treated each uncovered preservation function as requiring fresh adjacent prose. That rebuilt the model's preferred complete teaching unit around an owner-shaped core.

The resulting shape again approached:

`recognition -> positive pause -> adult move -> timed harm reduction -> present/old interpretation -> epistemic distinction -> safety close`

The exact owner block did not carry that complete arc by itself.

### Secondary: generator transfer failure

The model had literal owner teaching evidence available and could accurately describe several owner changes, but its new prose still defaulted to explicit semantic coverage, orderly progression, and loop closure. The fact that all three predicted realizations remained AI/medium supports the conclusion that alternative surface phrasing did not remove the latent completion prior.

### Preservation-process contribution

The preservation gate was applied too locally. `Function must survive` became, in practice, `missing function must receive a new adjacent sentence`. That is not required by the preservation protocol. Multiple units may map to one realization; a function may already be carried elsewhere in the natural section; movement/consolidation may be possible with authority; and owner supersession may alter local realization.

Before another rewrite, the correct discriminating step is a preservation-reallocation audit across the complete natural section for the R11 remainder functions.

## General method lesson

**Literal owner teaching can succeed locally and still fail to transfer when the model is tasked with restoring comprehensive semantic coverage around it.**

The failure is not just lexical or stylistic. The model's completion prior reappears through preservation work: every uncovered function becomes another reason to explain, qualify, or close.

For long practical guides, retrieval-first generation therefore needs a second question before writing any remainder:

> Is this function genuinely missing from the natural reader boundary, or are we about to create new model prose merely because the local paragraph no longer states it explicitly?

Only genuinely uncovered functions should enter generation. If the owner Human endpoint has changed the local rhetoric, do not reconstruct the old complete mini-manual around it by default.

## Production consequence

- Do not repeat Pangram on exact R11.
- Stop model-only rewrites of the same remainder architecture.
- Re-audit function coverage across the whole Episode 008 natural section before generating new prose.

This result does not isolate a token, sentence, emoji, punctuation mark, or one appended paragraph as causal. It demonstrates a failing exact combined boundary. The owner Human/high local block should not be reclassified as intrinsically AI-shaped merely because the larger R11 boundary failed.
