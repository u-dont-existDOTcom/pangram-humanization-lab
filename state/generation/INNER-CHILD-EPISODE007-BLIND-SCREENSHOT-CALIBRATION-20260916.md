## Agreement and misses

The blind audit correctly anticipated most of the long explanatory middle as model-shaped and correctly expected the late autobiography to fare better.

It missed/underweighted the immediate post-analogy explanatory paragraphs, which the screenshot marked AI, and incorrectly expected the no-self paragraph to be relatively Human-shaped; the screenshot marked it AI.

The opening provides the most useful detector/editorial disagreement: Pangram treats the visible top region as Human/high, while both Joel and the blind editorial audit still perceive model shape in some of its explanation.

## Supported lesson

This case reinforces an existing separation rather than creating a new detector rule:

> **Pangram Human/high is not an editorial exemption.** Detector status and model-shape/editorial quality are separate axes.

It also extends the Episode 006 listicle/register lesson one scale upward. Removing literal taxonomies can still leave repeated `principle -> explanation -> conclusion` mini-essays and therapist/explainer distance. A candidate can therefore be free of overt listicles while retaining model-shaped paragraph architecture.

For production work, after listicle cleanup audit the complete natural boundary for recursive mini-essay rhythm, matched paragraph jobs, polished closure, and whether the prose speaks from inside the reader's lived pressure rather than describing it from above.

## Causal limits

- This is screenshot segmentation plus an editorial blind audit, not a controlled factorial detector experiment.
- Do not infer that any individual phrase caused a detector region.
- Do not treat green spans as proof of natural authorship or stylistic cleanliness.
- Do not treat red spans as proof of AI authorship.
- The value of the case is calibration between independent pre-window editorial prediction and later detector segmentation.
