# Paragraph-isolated failure is stronger evidence than mixed-boundary failure

Date: 2026-09-19
Status: **ACTIVE METHOD LESSON**

For a difficult Inner Child subsection, model-written paragraphs were tested independently after the owner prohibited full-section masking.

Two materially different P1 realizations and two P3 realizations each returned Pangram 4.0 AI 1.0 in isolation.

This is stronger evidence of a generation problem than a mixed-boundary AI score because:
- no known-Human owner paragraph was present;
- no contextual anchor could mask or distort the result;
- each paragraph was long enough for the detector to act normally.

Production implication:
- after two materially different isolated paragraph attempts remain AI 1.0, stop same-method paraphrase;
- do not add more anti-pattern rules;
- seek a structurally different source of thought shape, such as owner rough cognition when the owner has explicitly offered it;
- test any repaired paragraph independently before reassembly.
