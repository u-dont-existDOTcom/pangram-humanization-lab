It's not that I can't access the love, but the love seems unsafe. My inner child is sitting there observing its grown-up self. It's saying, “Yeah, that doesn't look like things went well for us.” Then I find myself getting resentful at the child. I say, “Well, you're the one who was the child and you have the opportunity to grow up.” I end up at war with the younger version who didn't do what they were supposed to do. The older version, meanwhile, is trying to make this vow of protection and nurturing. The child is sitting there thinking, “Big fuckity whoopty doo. What are you gonna do for me?” Relaxation hasn't really affected that awkward, difficult time with the inner child for me. The problem, then, is not whether the love is there, but whether the child believes the adult offering it.
