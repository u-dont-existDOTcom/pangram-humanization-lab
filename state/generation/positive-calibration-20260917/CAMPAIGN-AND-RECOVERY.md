## The actual trajectory

The campaign began with a full natural section of roughly a thousand words. The early revisions changed broad regions, sequence, register and source explanation. The seventh revision produced useful Human/high islands despite substantial remaining AI. Later work retained those islands and narrowed its scope. Several intervening repairs were nearly flat. The final 39-word repair was the last operation, not the starting method.

The recorded progression includes approximately 40.24% displayed AI at R7, 39.67% at R8C, 29.74% at R8D, 94.65% displayed Human at R8E with a 39-word red residual, and the owner's report of 100% Human/high for R8F. These are historical result descriptions for their respective boundaries. Their UI word counts must not be confused with Markdown whitespace counts below.

The whole method is externally supervised convergence: broad repair when broad failure is present; externally informed regional selection; preservation of demonstrated progress; progressively narrower correction. It is not a claim that a model's favorable self-audit certifies the result.

## Identity defects discovered during recovery

The first three candidate JSON files contain `candidate_markdown_sha256_no_terminal_newline` values that do not match the actual stored `candidate_markdown` strings. This is not merely a comparison of detector-visible and Markdown surfaces: the discrepancy is in the explicitly named Markdown field.

The current stored strings are preserved and newly hashed above. Their historical metadata is not silently rewritten. Until the exact submission or original revision is reconciled, these early candidates may teach the visible historical revision trajectory but must not be used as hash-proven exact detector controls.
