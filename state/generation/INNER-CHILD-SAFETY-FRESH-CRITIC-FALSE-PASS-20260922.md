## Process correction

For Joel production humanization, fresh-context non-detection is non-gating unless:
1. the critic sees the target inside its natural reading boundary plus intended reader/local purpose;
2. it argues the strongest AI-shape case before cataloguing Human-facing features;
3. `mixed` findings are aggregated at paragraph/section scale;
4. protected cognition is kept separate from current realization—source necessity cannot by itself defend AI-shaped wording/topology;
5. reader model, why-now, and antecedent checks pass;
6. the same materially current critic configuration blindly accepts a same-register known-good control and detects a same-register known-bad control.

A bare `no definite AI tells` result is not detector-admission evidence.
