## Coherence recovery

The live thought is not `people should remember everyone is human` in the abstract. The useful observable is that **premature certainty means she has not found all of him yet**. If she has not encountered anything about him that bothers or disappoints her, that absence is evidence about how little of the person has been encountered, not proof of flawlessness.

The exes material is also not best realized as a deliberate diagnostic technique (`I show her this isn't a fairy tale`). Joel still loves women he loved before and sometimes talks about them because they remain on his mind. That concrete fact naturally tests whether the new partner can tolerate a real, complicated person without requiring Joel to stage a test.

The final asymmetry claim should stay concrete. The model's abstract balanced realization (`her whole inner world ... mine has to be cleaned up`) remained detector-AI. Recasting it as the immediate conversational problem—if Joel is supposed to listen to everything on her mind, he does not want to pretend something genuinely on his mind is absent—removed the packaged symmetry.

Also distinguish a person's naturally inconvenient reality from prose that frames the person as deliberately `showing` or `testing` a partner. For asymmetric disclosure, concrete conversational stakes can be superior to a polished mirrored formulation.
