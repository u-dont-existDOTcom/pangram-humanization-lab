# Production humanization preflight vs detector-research mode — 2026-08-23

Status: direct owner workflow correction, intended for promotion. Human editorial quality, semantic fidelity, provenance, and preservation remain blocking; this is not authorization to rewrite protected meaning merely to satisfy Pangram.

## Owner correction

During the Romance `Affection and the simmer` repair, Joel identified a systematic production-workflow error: paid Pangram calls were being used too mechanistically, with one-variable or narrowly controlled changes, even though successful humanization often requires recognizing several interacting model-shaped features and repairing the whole natural passage coherently before testing.

The owner-prescribed production loop is:

1. identify **all credible reasons the prose still looks AI-written**;
2. repair the whole natural boundary for those real editorial/model-shape problems in one coherent pass, subject to preservation and owner authority;
3. cold-read the resulting prose again and ask whether it still looks AI-written;
4. repeat the **unpaid editorial audit/revision** until the answer is effectively `no, not at all`—meaning the editor can no longer name a substantive model-shape problem they actually believe;
5. only then spend a Pangram call.

A useful paid-call readiness question is: **If Pangram returned AI on this candidate, would I be genuinely surprised?** If not, the candidate is not ready for a production detector call.

## Production mode is not detector science

For article production/humanization, Pangram is a scarce validation/checking instrument, not the drafting engine and not the default diagnostic oracle.

Before a production call, inspect the complete natural boundary for interacting problems such as:

- balanced therapist/explainer stance replacing the author's actual committed judgment;
- false symmetry, `both sides` completion, or comprehensive qualification;
- recap after the thought is already demonstrated;
- abstract diagnosis after a lived example already performs the conclusion;
- generic caveats added mainly to pre-empt objections;
- checklist/taxonomy structure that equalizes things the author does not treat equally;
- overly neat thesis → mechanism → synthesis packaging;
- source scaffolding that never becomes the author's thought;
- generic transitions or mini-conclusions;
- paragraph-by-paragraph equalized explanatory cadence;
- missing idiosyncratic stakes, concrete consequence, uncertainty, disagreement, or opinion that actually exists in the source;
- a stopping point that continues into responsible-sounding aftercare.

This is a **diagnostic sweep**, not a phrase blacklist. Fix only problems that are genuinely editorially/model-shaped in context, and preserve every protected claim/function unless owner authority allows a change.

A good production candidate may therefore change several interacting realization-level variables at once. That is desirable when the changes all follow from one coherent editorial diagnosis and pass the preservation gate.

## When controlled minimal pairs are appropriate

One-factor edits, factorial tests, and exact minimal pairs remain valuable in **detector-research mode**, where causal attribution itself is the goal.

They are also appropriate in production only when there is a genuinely narrow uncertainty whose answer will change the next editorial decision.

Do not spend a hard-capped production section budget on a ladder of mechanistic tests merely because controlled experiments are scientifically clean. Scientific identifiability and efficient article repair are different optimization goals.

Once a production candidate already works editorially, detector-research experiments may be run separately if the learning value justifies the calls and the evidence is clearly labeled as research rather than article authority.

## Evidence from Romance

The six-call Affection audit showed the failure mode:

- baseline established the section was detector-red;
- multiple calls changed many surface realizations while retaining substantially the same balanced relationship-advice architecture;
- one exact duplicate text was paid twice;
- a compressed diagnostic improved but was not preservation-valid as a complete section candidate;
- the sixth transition-boundary call became 100% Human but omitted much of Affection, so it localized context rather than solving the full section.

After reviewing the section as prose rather than as a sequence of detector variables, the assistant identified the broader model-shaped architecture: repeated balanced synthesis, `opposite failure`, `you need both`, checklist-like barometer explanation, and generalized shared-responsibility counseling. Joel accepted the resulting holistic editorial direction and reported that the proposed passage tested **100% Human, medium confidence**.

The immediately preceding Casual work supplied convergent evidence: removing/reworking isolated highlighted regions could make unchanged Human prose flip AI, while a coherent two-deletion consolidation later cleared Casual in the aggregate. Detector windows and individual sentences therefore should not be treated as independent knobs.

## Paid-call discipline

For production humanization:

- use free editorial reasoning/cold audits generously;
- prefer one fully considered candidate over several knowingly still-model-shaped candidates;
- every paid call must either test a candidate that has passed the holistic preflight or answer a narrow, decision-changing diagnostic question;
- never pay for exact duplicate text unless an intentional repeat is explicitly preregistered as detector research;
- preserve the hard section-call cap;
- preserve exact boundary/hash/cache/ledger safety;
- after a failed production call, diagnose the whole resulting boundary again rather than automatically toggling the highlighted sentence.

## Relationship to preservation

`Fix the whole thing` does **not** mean broad semantic freedom.

The authorized change surface is still bounded by owner authority and the humanization preservation gate. Holistic repair means addressing all genuine model-shaped realization/architecture problems **within the authorized semantic delta**, not deleting unique claims because they are inconvenient for Pangram.

If the remaining AI shape is caused by a protected idea that cannot be faithfully realized without fresh owner language, stop and request that language rather than manufacture a passing replacement.
