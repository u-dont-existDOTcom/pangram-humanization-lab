## Owner-language requests need reader-visible placement context

A narrow owner-language request is usable only when the owner can locate the thought in the article without reconstructing detector state. Name the exact article and heading, where inside the section the passage sits, what the preceding and following paragraphs do, the specific thought/job being supplied or replaced, and which neighboring functions remain untouched. Internal labels such as window numbers, word counts, preservation-unit IDs, or shorthand section names are not sufficient writing context. Richer placement context does not authorize broader editing; it makes the bounded authorial request intelligible.

## Production humanization uses holistic preflight before paid detector calls

For article production, Pangram is a scarce validation instrument, not the drafting engine. Before a paid call, inspect the complete natural boundary for **all** credible model-shaped problems—balanced therapist/explainer stance, false symmetry, recap/aftercare, checklist structure, generic caveats, overly neat thesis-mechanism-synthesis packaging, equalized explanatory cadence, weak stopping points, or missing situated author judgment. Repair the interacting problems coherently inside the owner-authorized preservation boundary, then cold-read again. Repeat the unpaid editorial audit until you can no longer name a substantive AI-shape problem you actually believe. A practical readiness question is: **if Pangram returned AI now, would I be genuinely surprised?** If not, do not spend the production call yet.

Controlled minimal pairs, factorial tests, and one-variable experiments remain valuable when causal detector research is itself the goal, or when one narrow uncertainty will genuinely change the next editorial decision. Do not spend a hard-capped production section budget on a mechanistic experimental ladder merely because it is scientifically clean. Scientific identifiability and efficient article repair are different goals. Every production call should test a fully considered candidate or answer a narrow decision-changing diagnostic question. Exact duplicate paid submissions remain forbidden unless an intentional repeat is explicitly preregistered as detector research. Preservation, fidelity, architecture, and owner authority remain blocking; `fix the whole thing` never licenses semantic loss.

## Stop broad model-only regeneration after replicated full-boundary failure

Somatic Therapies supplied a strong production stop case. On one stable `Job 2 → final high-intensity` natural boundary, two materially different candidates both passed semantic preservation with zero unexplained substantive deltas before measurement. r03 dismantled modality cards, duplicate evidence aftercare, recursive mini-essay closure, repeated map summary, and generic final moral. r04 then changed the realization more deeply without changing content: it exposed already-authorized authorial placement judgments, used live capacity questions, varied emphasis, and moved away from calm comprehensive clinical-guide mediation. Pangram 4.0 returned **AI 1.0, Human 0.0, High confidence, one complete-boundary AI window** for both 1,627-word r03 and 1,736-word r04.

The durable rule is not that these stylistic features are irrelevant in general. It is that once materially different, preservation-clean holistic production rewrites show no detector movement on the same large boundary, **do not treat more model-generated author imitation as a gradient**. Stop broad model-only variants, preserve the negative evidence, and look for genuinely fresh owner language or another independently human same-content realization. If none exists, keep article quality/fidelity separate from detector certification rather than manufacturing pseudo-owner prose. A full-boundary Pangram window in this situation is distributed localization evidence, not authority for sentence-level phrase fiddling. Keep the existing stable section-call ledger; do not reset the budget to evade the stop decision.

## Contextual AI windows do not authorize rewriting owner prose inside them

Somatic Therapies r07b is a production counterexample to sentence-level interpretation of Pangram's red windows. On the stable `Job 2 → final high-intensity` boundary, r03/r04 model-only candidates measured Human `0.0`; fresh-owner semantic reconstruction moved r05 to `0.0570` and r06 to `0.08345`; then a lower-dose r07b restored substantially more of Joel's current actual syntax/thought movement and moved the same natural boundary to Human `0.1626231521` / AI `0.8373768330`. The three High-confidence Human islands were the tapping/head-massage span, the Louka/Shaking opening, and the housemate/self-Brainspotting/restimulation span.

But the large High-confidence AI window began **inside near-direct owner wording**, at the continuation of Joel's TRE/more-angles/expertise thought, and then contained direct owner qigong, self-Brainspotting, EMDR-vs-Brainspotting, and neurological-dearmoring material along with inherited technical/safety scaffolding. Thus the window cannot be read as evidence that every sentence inside it is AI-shaped or should be rewritten. Treat it as contextual/distributed localization evidence. Higher-authority owner language inside the window remains protected unless there is an independent editorial/factual reason to change it. If the only available next move is to rewrite verified owner wording because Pangram painted the surrounding window red, stop and seek new owner realization or leave certification unresolved rather than erasing the source.

This also strengthens the edit-dose lesson: preserving more actual owner realization materially improved the whole-boundary score, but did not make Pangram a sentence-level authorship oracle. Detector windows, fidelity/provenance, and article architecture remain separate evidence planes.
