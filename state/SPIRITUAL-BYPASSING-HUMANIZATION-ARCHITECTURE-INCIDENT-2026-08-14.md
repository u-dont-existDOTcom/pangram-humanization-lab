# Spiritual Bypassing humanization architecture incident — 2026-08-14

## Finding

Humanization can create a dangerous editorial tunnel vision: once detector windows become the active target, locally plausible repairs can displace the article's governing architecture even while the exact full boundary eventually reaches 100% Human.

## What failed

The article title was `Spiritual Bypassing: Goenka "vipassana" retreats as a case study`, while the first body heading was `A Primer on Spiritual Bypassing`. During detector repair, the first section was progressively narrowed into a Goenka-specific prosecution. It no longer performed the heading's primer function, yet a reader-visible full article reached 100% Human on Pangram 4.0.

The detector pass therefore did not expose the editorial failure. Joel did.

A second failure followed from the same tunnel vision: when the remaining Pangram signal localized around the Dark Side transition, the workflow treated a later owner realization as if new authorial input were missing even though the relevant thought was already present elsewhere in the article. Later, a cold audit also caught an unauthorized deletion of the inherited line `Things can seize up.` that had been rationalized as merely inferable from the engine analogy.

## What repaired it

Four fresh model-written attempts to explain the primer remained detector-red and were editorially generic. The successful repair did not invent a fifth explanation. It searched article-wide for an existing owner realization and moved the actual mechanism into the section whose heading promised it:

- spiritual bypassing = trying to observe a painful feeling away, see it as insubstantial/not yours, and let it go instead of healing what is underneath;
- Goenka then functions as the case study because the retreat gives essentially one response to whatever surfaces: observe it and do not react;
- Joel's natural invitation to people who benefited from Goenka and his kindness/self-love healing lens remain in the primer;
- the later Dark Side passage no longer needs to redefine the mechanism.

The repaired primer reached 100% Human locally. After article-wide routing and removal of a heading-restatement bridge in the Loving Kindness section, the full reader-visible article reached 100% Human. A subsequent cold audit restored `Things can seize up.`; the fidelity-restored full article also reached 100% Human.

## Durable rule: architecture regression

Humanization never suspends ordinary editorial reasoning. Before the first detector call, and **after every detector-driven edit**, re-run an **architecture regression** from the whole article rather than from the last red window.

At minimum check:

1. object identity: article title vs section heading vs subheading;
2. each heading promise and whether the section actually fulfills it;
3. paragraph jobs and whether each paragraph changes the reader's state/question;
4. the reader's live question / curious-reader chain;
5. actor → action → object, mechanism, chronology, and causality;
6. protected rhetorical functions and owner-final claims;
7. article-wide duplication and whether a realization is in the wrong place;
8. whether the needed thought already exists as an **owner realization** elsewhere in the article/source pool before generating new explanatory prose or asking Joel again;
9. the real stopping point;
10. fidelity against the highest-authority baseline, including lines that may look inferable or redundant.

A red detector window may be a symptom of misplaced evidence, duplicate realization, or wrong section architecture rather than bad syntax. Search article-wide before paraphrasing locally.

A **100% Human** Pangram result is still editorially invalid if this architecture regression fails. Detector green cannot cure wrong heading fit, broken paragraph jobs, lost live questions, displaced owner thought, or fidelity loss.

## Scope

This is a general humanization workflow lesson, not a phrase rule and not a claim that every detector-red passage reflects architecture. Pangram remains a hard acceptance gate when Joel requests it, but editorial architecture and fidelity determine what candidate text is eligible to be accepted.
