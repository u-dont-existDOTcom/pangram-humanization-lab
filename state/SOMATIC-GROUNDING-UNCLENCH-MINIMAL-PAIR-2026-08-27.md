# Somatic grounding Human/AI minimal pair — 2026-08-27

Status: owner-reported detector evidence; exact-boundary local finding; not a universal lexical rule.

## Human backbone

Joel reported this exact passage as Pangram **Human / low confidence**:

> People talk about grounding as though it has to be a spiritual practice. Sometimes a foot massage is grounding for me. So is good food or music that relaxes your body. I suspect we make this stuff unnecessarily mystical because “my nervous system likes pleasant physical things” sounds too simple. That's kind of ironic, considering that simplicity is a huge part of grounding.

## One-phrase change

Joel then changed only:

- `music that relaxes your body`

to:

- `music that makes your body unclench`

and reported that the passage flipped back to **AI**.

## Interpretation

This is useful exact-boundary minimal-pair evidence against a simplistic `more vivid / more embodied = more Human` strategy. On this fixed Human backbone, the more vivid somatic realization `makes your body unclench` was detector-negative while the more ordinary `relaxes your body` was detector-positive.

A plausible editorial hypothesis is **model optimization pressure**: models often replace ordinary adequate language with more evocative, bodily, micro-concrete wording in an attempt to sound vivid or human. That optimization can itself be model-shaped. Human prose often leaves an ordinary phrase alone when it already says the thing.

Do not promote `unclench` to a banned word. The evidence is one exact boundary and one phrase substitution, reported at low confidence. Treat it as an interaction/local realization finding and look for counterexamples before generalizing.

## Broader comparison with the preceding model draft

The owner-Human rewrite also differed from the model draft in several non-isolated ways: it added a source-supported `for me`, shortened the sensory list, removed the model's tidy `But simple works` and soft-blanket/Sanskrit-name completion, and ended with an informal observation about the irony of overcomplicating grounding. Those differences remain bundled and should not be assigned causal weight from this one comparison.

## Production lesson

During humanization, do not automatically upgrade plain wording into more vivid somatic or sensory language. `Concrete` and `vivid` are not synonyms for `human`. Prefer the ordinary phrase when it is already exact enough, especially when the alternative looks like a model trying to demonstrate embodiment.
