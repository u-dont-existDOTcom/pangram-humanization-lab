## Current hypothesis

A promising higher-level explanation is not merely syntax but discourse stance. The candidate regression sentences tend toward polished, balanced, therapeutic/explanatory framing:
- neutral mechanism/explainer language (`Oxytocin, vasopressin...`),
- balanced concession (`You can both mean X and still Y`).

By contrast, the Human/protective material is more situated and committed:
- concrete consequence chain (next morning, pregnancy, child support, abortion),
- blunt questions and judgments,
- asymmetrical opinionated caveat (`If you’re both really numb or robotic about sex, maybe not.`).

This aligns with existing Romance lessons that model-shaped prose often over-completes, packages demonstrated dynamics into balanced conceptual summaries, or substitutes therapeutic/explanatory distance for lived thought movement.

However, the boundary is short and strongly interactive. Do **not** promote `balanced therapy language = AI` as a universal detector rule without controlled evidence across additional boundaries/authors. The immediate article implication is narrower: preserve the concrete/opinionated middle and test whether the two explanatory/balanced sentences are editorially redundant or better expressed elsewhere, rather than rewriting the whole Casual opening.
