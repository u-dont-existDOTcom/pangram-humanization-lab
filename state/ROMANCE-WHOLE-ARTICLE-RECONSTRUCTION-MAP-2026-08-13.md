## Whole governing movement

The article is a father's missing curriculum, learned through relationships: distinguish love from possession and physical attachment; decide whether and how to enter; preserve sight while chemistry closes it; let intimacy reveal rather than replace the inner life; understand the erotic and sacred experiences that make the dyad feel complete; then confront the fact that even the deepest pair cannot be a village or guarantee future feeling; choose a form honestly; repair or leave without falsifying what happened; return the knowledge to Bear.

The current article contains this movement, but four secondary architectures compete with it: a comprehensive dating checklist, a theory of masculine/feminine polarity, a critique of monogamy, and a repeated community manifesto. Reconstruction must subordinate those to the father's lived curriculum without flattening their odd specificity.

Primary article-level tensions that must remain open rather than solved into rules:

- eros is involuntary and particular; agape restrains it and gives it a landing pad, but cannot guarantee eros or dictate staying;
- intimacy can heal and reveal, and can also terrorize or retraumatize;
- slowness helps discernment but cannot reveal post-sex bonding or sexual fit;
- a practical partnership can be honest even though it feels wrong to Joel;
- safety and equality are necessary in Joel's account but do not themselves explain erotic charge;
- a Twin Flame experience can be sacred without proving a unique predestined person;
- community reduces dyadic overload but does not make an unready pair work;
- jealousy can be remembered insecurity and can also register a genuinely insecure relationship;
- an ending can be right without making the relationship false or beneficial in every respect.
