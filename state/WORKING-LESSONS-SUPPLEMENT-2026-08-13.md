# Working detector + humanization lessons supplement — 2026-08-13

Read this after `state/WORKING-LESSONS.md`. This file contains newer Romance findings that were not yet consolidated into that 2026-08-12 working file. Where they conflict, newer owner correction / controlled evidence wins.

Research state only. These are contextual editorial and detector findings, not phrase blacklists. Semantic sanity, fidelity, article function, paragraph progression, and stopping point outrank Pangram.

## 1. Semantic sanity can be the remaining detector bottleneck

Oxytocin case: the smooth opening `love hormone -> bonding should feel nice` did not logically match the paragraph, which was actually about whether increasing attachment/closeness feels safe. Repairing the premise to `getting more attached should make you feel closer and safer` aligned premise and mechanism and the normalized passage reached 100% Human.

Durable rule: before treating residual AI signal as realization/style, inspect whether the opening proposition actually implies the consequence the paragraph develops. A fluent premise can still ask the wrong question.

## 2. Whitespace findings need narrow causal scope

Pangram 4 showed real whitespace sensitivity in a controlled oxytocin pair: an accidental double space changed classification. Never exploit this. Normalize suspicious whitespace for direct lesson matrices/minimal pairs and promoted detector claims.

Do NOT rerun whole articles or unrelated publication sections merely because they contain odd spaces. The purpose is to validate causal detector lessons, not to chase article scores.

## 3. Stale provenance can mislead detector repair

In the casual-sex honesty case, stale corpus/provenance implied the full span was owner-final, but Joel corrected that only the middle paragraphs were his; the AI first/last paragraphs were assistant-produced. Current owner correction outranks old labels and old detector status.

Durable rule: when detector behavior conflicts sharply with provenance, verify the exact source span before treating text as locked. Owner-final, owner-accepted, detector-green, and natural owner authorship are distinct.

## 4. Replace abstract inner-state classification with lived discovery when that is the real thought

Casual-sex honesty case: the AI ending classified hope as conscious vs hidden and concluded that deliberate lying and self-deception were `the same lie at different depths`. The better thought was experiential: sometimes I know I hope it becomes love; sometimes I only discover that hope when it does not.

The final repaired boundary reached 100% Human. Do not promote the literal syntax as a rule. The supported editorial lesson is that a polished taxonomy of inner states can over-complete a dilemma the reader already understands; lived discovery through consequence may be the actual thought.

## 5. Recover the actual epistemic fact before adding moral explanation

Idealization case: `assume I'm human` was too empty. The useful observable was that if someone has not yet found anything about him that bothers her, she has not found all of him. The exes material also worked better as a naturally inconvenient fact (`they come up because I'm thinking about them`) than as an invented diagnostic strategy (`I'm showing her this isn't a fairy tale`).

The detector-red abstract reciprocal closure also localized cleanly: the concrete discovery sequence was Human while the polished mirrored principle remained AI. Recasting it as the actual conversational boundary made the whole span Human.

Durable rule: distinguish an inconvenient reality that naturally tests a relationship from prose that turns the author into someone deliberately staging a test. Prefer the concrete disagreement over an abstract balanced relational principle when that is where the live pressure actually sits.

## 6. Delete duplicate miniature arguments instead of humanizing them again

Progress-signals case: the article had already covered ex-blaming, current struggles, and recent conflicts. A later `polished confessions` mini-lesson repeated the same function. Four rewrites preserving that duplicated architecture stayed 100% AI. Removing the duplicate and keeping the unique local thought — spiritual depth / meditation benefit did not tell Joel how someone would act in a real conflict — produced 100% Human in context.

Durable rule: when a stubborn span restates an argument already established earlier, identify the unique local job and consolidate. This is not `shorter is more human`; it is functional de-duplication plus restored paragraph chronology.

## 7. Do not auto-expand a plural claim into a matched questionnaire

Doctor/patient case: after `both people need to look at their roles`, the model generated a symmetrical two-sided questionnaire, then a balanced acute-care exception, then a therapy-style script. The actual pressure was asymmetric and personal: helping feels good; being needed can feel good; Joel often genuinely had useful information; he also needed to ask whether the other person was trying to care for themself without him. The temporary-imbalance exception was already developed in the next subsection.

Durable rule: a plural setup does not obligate one neatly matched role analysis per actor. Follow the author's actual pressure, allow uneven emphasis, and delete duplicate exceptions/scripts already handled elsewhere.

## 8. Challenge invented continua/taxonomies before rewriting them

Monogamy/polyamory case: `murky middle between honest monogamy and polyamory` was conceptually wrong. Attraction to another person does not move a monogamous relationship toward polyamory. The span also repeated caveats already encoded by `often`, defended against a categorical claim the text had not made, and added tautological honesty-aftercare.

Longer rewrites preserving that explanatory architecture stayed 100% AI; the semantically narrower unique claim went 100% Human.

Durable rule: before humanizing a red passage, ask whether it is manufacturing a continuum/taxonomy or redundant qualification that the underlying thought does not need. Delete the false conceptual structure instead of polishing it.

## 9. Local Human status does not compose into global Human status

Artificial-checklist case is strong evidence. Nearly every practical thought could be realized as 100% Human alone: conversation/scenarios, pacing, discomfort, friends, decision/certainty, inner practices. Yet recombining those locally Human pieces into a comprehensive `What doing it consciously looks like` recap repeatedly returned to 100% AI. Bullets vs paragraphs did not solve it; causal-chain and non-taxonomic rewrites did not solve it; even a unique-only recap remained AI.

Some pairs composed and some did not (`pacing + discomfort` Human; `friends + practices` AI), showing a higher-order architecture interaction rather than a simple sentence property.

Durable rule: a detector-red section can consist entirely of locally Human thoughts. Inspect whether the section itself exists mainly to complete conceptual space. If so, route genuinely unique functions to the live questions that need them and delete the recap architecture.

## 10. Function routing can repair both coherence and detector behavior

For the removed recap, successful routing was:
- epistemic provenance/certainty -> directly under `Doing it consciously`;
- scenario-vs-abstract-answer method -> `Starting on the right foot / The window of clarity`;
- pacing heavy conversations -> same window section;
- discomfort as information -> same window section after negative-side/stress material;
- romantic-vs-long-term decision and premature certainty -> `If slow isn't realistic for you`;
- cohabitation/check-ins, mutual friends, and inner practices -> no recap repetition because already developed in their natural sections.

The resulting altered boundaries tested 100% Human in Pangram 4, including the extended `Why all of this sounds artificial -> If you're already in it` boundary through the trust/dishonesty material.

Durable rule: when a recap is model-shaped, preserve substantive functions by routing them to the reader's live questions rather than preserving the recap container.

## 11. Headings should not be followed by prose that merely re-explains the heading

`If you're already in it` improved when it stopped with the category-of-reader setup and began directly with the lived governing lesson: one person cannot create mutual honesty. The heading already performed the categorization.

Durable rule: after a sufficiently explicit heading, ask whether the first paragraph advances the thought or merely restates who the section is for.

## 12. Architecture outranks detector-green local prose

The current Romance article contains passages humanized with an earlier process that can pass Pangram while barely flowing. This is a different failure mode from detector-red AI prose: sentence-level edits preserved local Human realization but damaged the movement that made one thought generate the next.

For full-article reconstruction, use:
- CURRENT article as authoritative for current claims, owner-final language, corrections, memories, links/media, and later insights;
- ORIGINAL AI article only as a thought-architecture / flow source where it preserved a better governing movement.

Do not `humanize the old AI article`. Recover the best movement of thought and rebuild the current material inside it. If neither version has good architecture, reconstruct from the richest Joel/source pool.

This is a working reconstruction principle, not yet a Pangram causal claim.

## 13. Parallelize diagnosis, not final prose ownership

For large reconstruction work, multiple agents can independently audit architecture, fidelity/provenance, curious-reader chain, redundancy/placement, and semantic sanity. One Lead Editor must own the whole-article architecture and assemble prose sequentially.

Do not assign independent subagents final ownership of different sections and concatenate them. That risks reproducing the same locally-good/globally-discontinuous failure mode.

Review agents should diagnose defects and provide evidence; the Lead Editor resolves them against the unified architecture and current provenance.

## Non-conclusions

Do not infer universal rules such as:
- fragments are Human;
- shorter is Human;
- `Maybe` is Human;
- balanced syntax is always AI;
- lists are AI;
- three-item lists are AI;
- contractions are always Human;
- whitespace tricks should be used;
- Pangram green proves natural authorship.

Treat detector behavior as contextual and nonlinear. Promote only the editorial principle justified independently of the score, with exact controls retained for the detector claim.

### Restore owner authority after a detector pass
A complete detector pass does not authorize keeping a broader model rewrite. Cold-audit the passing text against the highest-authority owner baseline and try to restore owner-selected wording and function. If exact restoration reopens the detector signal, localize the interaction and make the smallest function-preserving repair that is actually needed, then retest the exact delivery boundary. This separates detector compliance from prose authority; it is not a phrase rule.
