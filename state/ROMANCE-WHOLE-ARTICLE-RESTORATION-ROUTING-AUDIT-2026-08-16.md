- ending can be right without making the love false or the relationship beneficial in every respect.

- **How to find:** meeting and observation environment; other people know the person before the date-performance becomes the whole evidence set.
- **Starting:** early warning that the couple is beginning to close around itself; short pointer only.

- **Two Pillars:** primary structural proof that two people cannot supply an entire village or impartial witness network.
- **Choosing:** community changes what the relationship is being asked to carry and is one relationship-container option.
- **Why Artificial:** explains why conscious instructions are needed in the absence of an ambient social container.
- **If Already:** community becomes an intervention after the dyad is already sealed.
- **Children:** village provides adult continuity not indexed entirely to parents' romance.
- **Ending:** mutual friends/community become witnesses during conflict and competing memories.
- **Tough Love:** cultural synthesis; modern isolation is part of the environment in which private pathology grows.

Do not deduplicate these merely because `community`, `friends`, or `outside support` recur. Cut only where a section starts re-proving Two Pillars' full mechanism.

The error is **exact or near-exact centralization**, not conceptual recurrence. Slow remains untouched. Vows uses its owner r27/r28 story-first argument and does not import the Aug. 13 extra contract sentences merely to make the section self-contained.

Different object, different time:
