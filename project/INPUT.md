The free-will question bothered me for almost the same reason. If everything arises from conditions, my choices do too. So who's making them, and based on what? I don't think I've ever even seen the phrase “free will” in the suttas. AN 6.63 says intention is kamma and that contact is its source; AN 3.61 rejects views that would make effort meaningless. Choices happen and matter. They become conditions for what happens next. Where I get lost-ish is the idea of a chooser somehow outside all those conditions.
