I asked my dad about sex when I was five, and he briefly explained:: “Sex is what you do when you are older and you find a friend you want to have children with.”
It was a good tip, but it left out the whole question of romance: why that friend rather than one of the others, what problems people have in relationships, and how to prevent or handle them.
I can't expect my dad to have developed a whole guide to all that, because that wasn't his main mission. So I ended up learning most of it the hard way, and this is what I'd like to pass on to my kids, although by the time they read it, the existence of natural humans, romance, love, might be long forgotten, for all I know.
This guide isn't universally applicable for everyone even now, since it comes from my particular life experience (including influences I've had from friends & research). I’m a straight man, and I’m writing from relationships I’ve had with women, so she and her are going to show up a lot. Most of this has nothing specifically to do with being male or straight. The Primal Attraction section is the exception, because there I’m specifically talking about the man-woman dynamic I’ve actually lived.
