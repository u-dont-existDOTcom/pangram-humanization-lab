So I reread the draft and picture the kid who wrote that first angry sentence still gripping the pencil, because he is hurt and at least the sentence is doing something about it. The adult hand doesn’t take the pencil away or slap his wrist; it just rests nearby and says, okay, try that line again. Which can feel like backing down, honestly, especially if the bad wording was the only part willing to stand up for you. But then you read it once more and pause over one phrase that seems almost pleased with how much it might sting, though maybe that isn’t quite fair either, and the adult is still there, waiting, not quite sure what to keep.
